# Art. 406 codice penale - Delitti contro i culti ammessi nello Stato

## Summary
Article 406 of the Italian Penal Code, now abrogated (L. 24 febbraio 2006, n. 85), provided for reduced penalties when the offenses described in articles 403, 404, and 405 were committed against a "cult allowed in the State." It was part of the original regime protecting religious sentiment, later replaced by a broader protection of all religious confessions.

## Key Details
- Abrogated by art. 9 of Law 24 February 2006, n. 85.
- Applied to offenses under [[art403-cp]] (offenses against religion or worship), [[art404-cp]] (insults to a religion), and [[art405-cp]] (disturbance of religious functions).
- The penalty reduction was general ("la pena è diminuita") for acts against any state-recognized cult.
- The article reflected the pre-2006 constitutional framework that distinguished between the Catholic religion and "cults admitted in the State."
- After abrogation, all confessions are treated equally under the revised articles.
