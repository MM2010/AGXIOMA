# Art. 293 codice penale - Circostanza aggravante

## Summary
Art. 293 of the Italian Penal Code (abrogated by Law 24 February 2006, n. 85) provided an aggravating circumstance for the crimes described in the two preceding articles (artt. 291 e 292 c.p.) when committed by an Italian citizen abroad. The penalty was increased pursuant to the general rules on penalty increases (cf. [[art64-cp]]). The article was part of the chapter on crimes against the internal personality of the State (Titolo I, Capo II).

## Key Details
- **Abrogation**: Art. 293 was repealed by art. 12 of Law 24 February 2006, n. 85.
- **Original text**: "Nei casi indicati dai due articoli precedenti, la pena è aumentata se il fatto è commesso dal cittadino in territorio estero."
- **Scope**: The aggravating circumstance applied only to Italian citizens who committed one of the crimes under art. 291 (attentato contro la personalità interna dello Stato) or art. 292 (attentato contro la Costituzione) while abroad.
- **Relation to general rules**: The increase of penalty was governed by the general provisions of [[art64-cp]] (aumento di pena).
- **Legislative context**: The article belonged to the section "Dei delitti contro la personalità interna dello Stato" and was preceded by art. 292 and followed by art. 294.
- **Reason for abrogation**: The 2006 law significantly reformed the crimes against the personality of the State, eliminating the specific aggravating circumstance for extraterritorial commission.
