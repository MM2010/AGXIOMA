# Art. 871 codice civile - Norme di edilizia e di ornato pubblico

## Summary
Article 871 of the Italian Civil Code establishes the general principle that building and ornamental public rules are governed by special legislation and municipal building regulations. It further mandates that special laws provide the rules to be observed for constructions in seismic areas, aiming to ensure structural safety and urban decorum.

## Key Details
- The article is located in Book III (Property), Title II (Property Ownership), Chapter II (Land Ownership), Section V (Building Property).
- Construction rules are determined by **special law** (legge speciale) and **municipal building regulations** (regolamenti edilizi comunali).
- For seismic zones, the special law dictates additional construction requirements.
- The norm serves as a framework, delegating detailed technical and administrative regulation to subordinate sources.
- It reflects the principle that building activity is subject to public-interest constraints beyond private property rights.
