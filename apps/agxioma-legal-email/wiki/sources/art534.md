# Art. 534 codice penale - Sfruttamento di prostitute

## Summary
Art. 534 of the Italian Penal Code, titled "Sfruttamento di prostitute" (Exploitation of prostitutes), originally criminalised the act of living – even in part – on the earnings a woman obtained from prostitution. The provision was placed in Book II, Title IX (Crimes against public morality and good customs), Chapter II (Offences against modesty and sexual honour). It was abrogated by art. 3 of Law 20 February 1958, n. 75, which overhauled prostitution legislation, replacing the previous regulatory framework with an abolitionist approach.

## Key Details
- Abrogated by **art. 3 of l. 20 febbraio 1958, n. 75**.
- Original text: punished anyone who maintained themselves (even partially) by exploiting the earnings of a woman from prostitution.
- Penalty: imprisonment from two to six years and a fine from 1,000 to 10,000 lire.
- Applied only when the act did not constitute a more serious crime (clausola di sussidiarietà).
- Located in Book II, Title IX, Chapter II of the Penal Code.
- The article exclusively referred to exploitation of women, reflecting the gender-specific language of the time.
- Its abrogation aligns with the shift toward suppressing all forms of profiting from prostitution, rather than regulating brothels.
