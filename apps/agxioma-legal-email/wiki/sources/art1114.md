# Art. 1114 codice civile - Divisione in natura

## Summary
Article 1114 of the Italian Civil Code, located in Book III (Property), Title VII (Communion), Chapter I (Communion in General), establishes the general rule that the division of common property must be carried out in kind (*in natura*) whenever the thing can be conveniently divided into parts corresponding to the shares of the participants. This provision is the cornerstone of the principle of material division, limiting recourse to sale and distribution of proceeds only when physical division is impossible or inconvenient, as further regulated by subsequent articles ([[art718-cc]] and following).

## Key Details
- The article is part of the discipline on *comunione* (co-ownership).
- It prescribes that division shall take place *in natura* if the thing can be *comodamente divisa* (conveniently divided).
- The parts must correspond to the quotas (*quote*) of the participants.
- The concept of *comoda divisibilità* is determined on a case-by-case basis, considering the nature of the asset, its economic value, and the possibility of partitioning without disproportionate loss or inconvenience.
- If the thing is not conveniently divisible, the division must be carried out by sale and distribution of the proceeds (see [[art718-cc]]).
- The article implicitly applies to both voluntary and judicial divisions.
- It reflects the legislator's preference for preserving the functional and economic unity of the asset only when division is impracticable.
