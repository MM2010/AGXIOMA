# Art. 921 codice civile - Consorzi coattivi

## Summary
Art. 921 of the Italian Civil Code provides that, in the case referred to in Article 918, a consortium may also be compulsorily established by the administrative authority for the purpose of ensuring a better utilization of waters. The rules governing formation and operation follow those applicable to land improvement consortia. The consortium may also proceed with the expropriation of individual rights upon payment of due indemnities.

## Key Details
- Applicable to the situation described in [[art918]] (water use coordination among multiple owners).
- The consortium can be created ex officio by the administrative authority, not only by voluntary agreement.
- Purpose: achieving a better and more efficient use of water resources.
- The formation and functioning are governed by the same regulations as consorzi di miglioramento fondiario (land improvement consortia).
- The consortium has the power to expropriate individual rights (e.g., water rights) with payment of appropriate compensation.
- This article is located in Book III, Title II, Chapter II, Section IX (Waters) of the Civil Code.
