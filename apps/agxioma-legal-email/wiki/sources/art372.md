# Art. 372 codice penale – Falsa testimonianza

## Summary
Article 372 of the Italian Penal Code criminalises false testimony (falsa testimonianza). It punishes any witness who, before a judicial authority or the International Criminal Court, makes a false statement, denies the truth, or conceals, in whole or in part, what they know about the facts on which they are examined. The offence protects the integrity of judicial proceedings and carries a penalty of imprisonment from two to six years.

## Key Details
- Applies to witnesses deposing before the Italian judicial authority or the International Criminal Court (see also [[art244-cpp]], [[art245-cpp]], and procedural rules [[art194-cpp]]–[[art198-cpp]], [[art468-cpp]], [[art497-cpp]]–[[art499-cpp]]).
- The prohibited conduct includes: (a) affirming the false; (b) denying the true; (c) remaining silent, in whole or in part, about facts known and asked.
- Punishment: reclusione (imprisonment) from 2 to 6 years.
- The crime is consummated at the moment the false statement is made or the truth is withheld.
- It is a dolus generic crime: the witness must act with the intention to deceive.
- The article does not require that the false testimony actually influences the outcome of the proceeding; the mere act of mendacity suffices.
- Special procedural protections apply, such as the right to remain silent (self-incrimination) which can limit the scope of the duty to testify truthfully.
