# Art. 612 - Minaccia

## Summary
Article 612 of the Italian Penal Code punishes the act of threatening another person with an unjust harm. The basic form is punishable by a fine of up to €1,032 and requires a complaint (querela) from the victim. If the threat is serious (grave) or made in one of the ways listed in Article 339 (e.g., with a weapon, in writing, or with disguise), the penalty is imprisonment up to one year; in these aggravated cases, prosecution proceeds ex officio under certain conditions.

## Key Details
- **Objective element**: threatening another with an *unjust harm* (any damage that is not legally justified).
- **Simple threat**: fine up to €1,032; proceeding requires a querela (complaint) from the injured party.
- **Aggravated threat** (grave or via means under [[art339]]): imprisonment up to one year.
- **Ex officio prosecution** applies when:
  - the threat is made in the modes of Article 339 (e.g., publicly, with a weapon);
  - the threat is grave and accompanied by aggravating circumstances with special effect (excluding recidivism);
  - the victim is incapable due to age or infirmity.
- **The threat must be externalised** (verbal, written, symbolic, or by conduct) and capable of intimidating the victim.
- **Subjective element**: general intent (dolo generico) – the perpetrator must intend to communicate the threat of an unjust harm.
