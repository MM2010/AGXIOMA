# Art. 672 c.p. – Omessa custodia e mal governo di animali

## Summary
Art. 672 of the Italian Penal Code (Book III, Title I, Capo I, Section II) punishes as a contravention the failure to properly guard dangerous animals or the entrusting of their custody to an inexperienced person. It also covers the abandonment of draft, pack or race animals in open places without supervision, even if tied, and the deliberate incitement or frightening of animals in a way that endangers public safety. The penalty is an administrative fine from €25 to €258.

## Key Details
- The article is a *contravvenzione* concerning public safety (*incolumità pubblica*).
- It applies to anyone who owns dangerous animals and leaves them free, fails to guard them with due precautions, or entrusts custody to an inexperienced person.
- Under paragraph 2, the same fine applies to:
    1. Abandoning draft, pack or race animals in open places without custody, even if tied, or hitching or driving them in a manner that endangers public safety.
    2. Entrusting such animals to an inexperienced person.
    3. Inciting or frightening animals so as to endanger the safety of persons (liability under [[art2052-cc]] for civil damages is referenced in the original source).
- The penalty is an administrative sanction (not a criminal fine under the Penal Code, though historically it might have been different).
- No imprisonment or criminal record; purely a pecuniary administrative penalty.
