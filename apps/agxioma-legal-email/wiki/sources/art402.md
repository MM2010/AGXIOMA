# Art. 402 codice penale - Vilipendio della religione dello Stato

## Summary
Art. 402 of the Italian Penal Code (now abrogated) criminalized the public vilification of the religion of the State — i.e., Catholicism, which at the time held a privileged status. Punishable by up to one year of imprisonment, the provision was part of the original 1930 Codice Penale and reflected the former concordatory regime. It was abrogated by Legge 24 febbraio 2006, n. 85 as part of a broader reform that replaced discriminatory protections for the State religion with equal safeguards for all religious confessions under articles 403–405.

## Key Details
- **Original text:** "Chiunque pubblicamente vilipende la religione dello Stato è punito con la reclusione fino a un anno."
- **Application:** Only protected the Catholic religion, considered the "religion of the State" under the Lateran Pacts.
- **Punishment:** Reclusione (imprisonment) up to one year.
- **Status:** Abrogated by L. 24 febbraio 2006, n. 85 (art. 57, comma 1, lett. a).
- **Replaced by:** Articles 403–405 of the Penal Code, which extend protection to all legally recognized religious confessions.
- **Context:** Belongs to Title IV ("Dei delitti contro il sentimento religioso e contro la pietà dei defunti"), Capo I ("Dei delitti contro le confessioni religiose") — renamed after the 2006 reform.
- **Historical significance:** Emblematic of the pre‑2006 asymmetry between the State religion and other faiths, later harmonized with constitutional principles of equal freedom (art. 8, 19 Cost.).
