# Art. 588 codice penale - Rissa

## Summary
This article of the Italian Penal Code punishes participation in a brawl (rissa). The base penalty is a fine up to €2,000. If the brawl results in death or personal injury, the penalty for mere participation increases to imprisonment from six months to six years, even if the death or injury occurs immediately after the brawl and as a consequence.

## Key Details
- The crime consists of taking part in a rissa (a violent physical confrontation between multiple persons).
- Basic penalty: fine up to €2,000 (multa).
- Aggravated penalty (reclusione da 6 mesi a 6 anni) applies if a participant dies or suffers a personal injury (art. 582-583).
- The aggravated penalty also applies if the death or injury occurs "immediately after" the brawl and in consequence of it.
- The penalty is imposed for the mere fact of participation, regardless of who caused the death/injury.
- The article is found in Book II, Title XII (crimes against the person), Chapter I (crimes against life and individual safety).
