# Art. 133 bis c.p. – Condizioni economiche e patrimoniali del reo

## Summary
Art. 133 bis of the Italian Penal Code mandates that, when determining the amount of a fine (multa or ammenda), the judge must take into account the offender’s economic and patrimonial conditions in addition to the general criteria of art. 133. It further empowers the judge to increase the statutory amount up to three times or reduce it down to one third if the maximum appears ineffective or the minimum excessively burdensome in light of those conditions. This provision aims to ensure proportionality and effectiveness of pecuniary penalties.

## Key Details
- Applicable to both *multa* (penalty for crimes) and *ammenda* (penalty for contraventions).
- The judge **must** consider the offender’s economic and patrimonial conditions alongside the criteria of art. 133.
- Discretionary adjustment: the statutory amount may be increased up to threefold or decreased to one third.
- Adjustment is allowed only if the maximum is deemed **inefficace** (ineffective) or the minimum **eccessivamente gravosa** (excessively burdensome) due to the offender’s financial situation.
- The purpose is to individualise the pecuniary penalty, avoiding a disproportionate hardship or an insignificant sanction.
- This provision does not apply to other types of penalties (e.g., detention) or to fixed fines without a statutory range.
