# Art. 738 codice civile - Limiti della collazione per il coniuge

## Summary
The article establishes an exception to the collation obligation: donations of modest value (modico valore) made to the spouse are not subject to collation, meaning they are not brought into the division of the estate. This provision, referring to [[art783-cp]] for the definition of modest value, protects the spouse's right to retain such gifts without reducing their share in the succession. It is part of the rules on collation (Capo II, Titolo IV, Libro II) of the Italian Civil Code.

## Key Details
- **Norm:** Art. 738 c.c. – "Non sono soggetti a collazione le donazioni di modico valore fatte al coniuge."
- **Scope:** Only donations of modest value to the spouse are exempt; larger donations to the spouse remain subject to collation.
- **Reference:** The definition of "modico valore" is found in [[art783-cp]] (donations of modest value do not require the formal act of donation).
- **Position:** Located in Book II – Successions, Title IV – Division, Chapter II – Collation.
- **Ratio:** Protects the spouse's economic independence and the stability of marital gifts, avoiding the need to return small gifts to the estate.
- **Relation:** Exception to the general collation rule ([[art724-cp]] etc.) that requires heirs to bring gifts received from the decedent into the division.
