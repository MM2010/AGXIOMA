# Art. 574 bis codice penale - Sottrazione e trattenimento di minore all'estero

## Summary
This article (introduced by art. 8, law 15 July 2009, n. 94) criminalizes the abduction or retention of a minor abroad, depriving the parent exercising parental responsibility (or the guardian) of the exercise of that responsibility. The basic offense is punishable by 1 to 4 years of imprisonment; if the minor is 14 or older and consents, the penalty is reduced to 6 months to 3 years. When the perpetrator is a parent, the conviction also entails suspension from parental responsibility. The provision protects the minor's right to maintain a relationship with both parents and prevents international child abduction.

## Key Details
- The conduct consists in taking or keeping a minor abroad against the will of the parent or guardian having parental responsibility.
- The crime requires that the abductor “impede in whole or in part” the exercise of parental responsibility.
- The article applies only when the fact does not constitute a more serious crime (e.g., kidnapping).
- Second paragraph: if the minor has reached 14 years of age and gives valid consent, the penalty is reduced.
- Third paragraph: if the perpetrator is a parent of the minor, conviction automatically entails suspension of parental responsibility.
- The offense is placed within Title XI (crimes against the family), Chapter IV (crimes against family assistance).
- The article was introduced to implement international obligations (e.g., Hague Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child Abduction).
