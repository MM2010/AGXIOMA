# Art. 872 codice civile - Violazione delle norme di edilizia

## Summary
Art. 872 of the Italian Civil Code governs the civil consequences of violating building regulations (norme di edilizia) referred to in art. 871. It separates the administrative sanctions (governed by special laws) from the private law remedies: the injured party is entitled to damages, and may also request restoration of the original state (*riduzione in pristino*) when the violated rules belong to the following section (Sezione VI) or are recalled by it.

## Key Details
- The article establishes a dual regime: administrative consequences are reserved to special legislation; civil consequences are regulated by the Civil Code.
- Any person who suffers damage due to the violation has a right to compensation (risarcimento del danno).
- In addition to damages, the injured party may demand *riduzione in pristino* (restoration to the prior condition) under [[art2933]] when the infringement concerns norms contained in the subsequent section (Sezione VI: Delle distanze nelle costruzioni) or norms recalled by that section.
- The *riduzione in pristino* remedy is not automatic for all building violations; it is limited to specific rules on distances and related provisions.
- The article does not define the violation itself; that is covered by art. 871. Art. 872 only addresses the consequences.
- The right to request *riduzione in pristino* is subject to the general rules on judicial enforcement (art. 2933 c.c.).
- The provision confirms the private nature of building rules protections, separate from public law enforcement.
