# Art. 183 codice penale - Concorso di cause estintive

## Summary
This article, part of the common provisions on the extinction of crime and penalty, establishes the rules for handling concurrent causes of extinction. It prioritizes causes that extinguish the crime over those that extinguish the penalty, and provides a hierarchy for multiple causes occurring at different times or simultaneously, ensuring only the most favorable effects persist.

## Key Details
- Causes of extinction of the crime ([[art150-cp]]–[[art170-cp]]) or of the penalty ([[art171-cp]]–[[art181-cp]]) operate at the moment they occur.
- When a cause extinguishing the crime and a cause extinguishing the penalty concur, the cause extinguishing the crime prevails, even if it occurred later.
- If multiple causes of extinction (of crime or penalty) occur at different times, the earlier cause extinguishes the crime or penalty entirely; subsequent causes only extinguish effects not already eliminated.
- If multiple causes occur simultaneously, the most favorable cause applies for extinction; remaining effects are then subject to the same rule as for successive causes (i.e., subsequent cause extinguishes what remains).
- The provision ensures no double counting of extinction effects while maximizing the benefit of the most favorable cause.
