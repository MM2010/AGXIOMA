# Art. 495 codice penale - Falsa attestazione o dichiarazione a un pubblico ufficiale sulla identità o su qualità personali proprie o di altri

## Summary
Article 495 of the Italian Penal Code (Codice Penale) punishes anyone who falsely declares or attests to a public official the identity, status, or other personal qualities of oneself or another person. The crime falls under the chapter on personal falsehoods (Capo IV – Della falsità personale) within Title VII (Delitti contro la fede pubblica). The penalty is imprisonment from one to six years, with a minimum of two years in specific aggravated circumstances, such as declarations in civil status acts or false identity statements made by a suspect or person under investigation to judicial authorities, or when the false declaration leads to a criminal record entry under a false name.

## Key Details
- **Element**: Falsely declaring or attesting identity, status, or other personal qualities to a public official (either for oneself or for another).
- **Base penalty**: Reclusione da uno a sei anni (imprisonment from 1 to 6 years).
- **Aggravating circumstances (minimum 2 years)**:
  - Declarations made in acts of civil status (atti dello stato civile).
  - The false declaration about one’s own identity, status, or personal qualities is made by an imputato (defendant) or a person under investigation (persona sottoposta ad indagini) to the judicial authority.
  - The false declaration causes a criminal decision to be registered under a false name in the judicial record (casellario giudiziale).
- **Legal classification**: Crime against public faith (fede pubblica), specifically personal falsehood.
- **Relation to other offenses**: May overlap with false declarations to a public official (art. 483 c.p.) and other falsity crimes.
