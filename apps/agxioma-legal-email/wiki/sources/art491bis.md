# Art. 491 bis codice penale - Documenti informatici

## Summary
Art. 491-bis of the Italian Penal Code extends the provisions on falsity in public acts (Capo III, Titolo VII) to public electronic documents that have probative value. It ensures that any forgery or alteration of such digital documents is treated with the same severity as the falsification of traditional public acts, protecting the reliability of digital instruments used in legal and administrative contexts.

## Key Details
- Introduces criminal liability for falsifications committed on "documento informatico pubblico" (public electronic document).
- The document must have "efficacia probatoria" (probative value) under Italian law.
- Applies the same penalties and rules from the chapter on falsity in acts (articles 476–493) that govern public acts.
- Does **not** apply to private electronic documents or those without probative force.
- Considers the unique vulnerabilities of digital documents (e.g., alteration, deletion, insertion).
- Article number: 491-bis (often written as Art. 491bis cp).
- Enacted to align criminal law with technological evolution and the increasing use of IT documents in public administration.
