# Art. 227 codice penale - Riformatori speciali

## Summary
Art. 227 of the Italian Penal Code governs the assignment of minors to special reformatories or to special sections within ordinary reformatories. It provides that when the law orders placement in a judicial reformatory without requiring a prior assessment of social dangerousness (as in articles 224, 225, 226, 232), the minor is assigned to a special facility or a dedicated section. Additionally, a minor who reveals himself as particularly dangerous during an ordinary stay may be transferred to a special section or facility.

## Key Details
- The article applies only when the law mandates the measure without needing to ascertain the minor's social dangerousness.
- Assignment is to a "stabilimento speciale" (special establishment) or a "sezione speciale" (special section) of ordinary establishments.
- It also covers the case of a minor already in an ordinary reformatory who becomes particularly dangerous, allowing transfer to a special section/establishment.
- The provision is part of the section "Disposizioni speciali" under personal security measures (Titolo VIII, Capo I, Sezione II).
- Relevant cross-references within the Code: the measure is linked to the commission of certain crimes by minors (articles 224–226, 232).
