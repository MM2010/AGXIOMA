# Art. 1182 codice civile - Luogo dell'adempimento

## Summary
Art. 1182 of the Italian Civil Code determines the place where an obligation must be performed when not otherwise agreed by the parties, established by usage, or inferable from the nature of the performance (see [[art1174]]). It sets default rules: for delivery of a specific thing, at the place where the thing was when the obligation arose; for monetary obligations, at the creditor's domicile at maturity (with a right of the debtor to pay at their own domicile if the creditor's change makes performance more burdensome); and for all other cases, at the debtor's domicile at maturity. These rules allocate risk and convenience between creditor and debtor and interact with provisions on default ([[art1219]]) and specific types of delivery ([[art1510]], [[art1774]]).

## Key Details
- The place of performance is determined first by agreement, usage, nature of the performance, or other circumstances; only if these are absent do the statutory rules apply.
- **Specific thing** (certa e determinata): must be delivered where the thing was located when the obligation arose (e.g., a painting at the gallery where it was stored).
- **Sum of money**: must be paid at the creditor's domicile at the time of maturity (portable obligation). If the creditor moves after the obligation arose and makes payment more burdensome, the debtor may, after declaring it, pay at their own domicile.
- **Other cases** (any prestation not falling above): must be performed at the debtor's domicile at maturity (queryable obligation).
- The "domicile" refers to the legal domicile or residence; changes affect the place of performance only if they occur before maturity.
- The debtor's right to shift payment location due to creditor's domicile change is a procedural protection against unilateral worsening of the debtor's burden.
- Failure to perform in the correct place may constitute late payment (mora) under [[art1219 n. 3]].
- Specific additional rules exist for delivery contracts ([[art1510]]) and deposits ([[art1774]]), referenced in the article's source commentary.
