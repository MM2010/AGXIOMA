# Art. 977 codice civile – Enfiteusi costituite dalle persone giuridiche

## Summary

The article extends the provisions of the preceding articles on emphyteusis (a long-term real right over land) to emphyteuses established by legal persons (e.g., corporations, foundations). Unless special laws provide otherwise, the same rules apply when a legal person acts as grantor or grantee.

## Key Details

- Article 977 is part of **Title IV – Dell’enfiteusi** in Book III of the Italian Civil Code.
- It states that the rules on emphyteusis (articles 957–976) are applicable mutatis mutandis to creations of emphyteusis by legal persons.
- The principle of universal application is subject to exceptions expressly provided by special laws (e.g., agrarian or religious legislation).
- Legal persons include both private (e.g., associations, companies) and public (e.g., state entities) bodies, unless special laws limit capacity.
- The article ensures uniformity of the institute regardless of the nature of the person involved (natural or legal).
