# Art. 748 codice civile - Miglioramenti, spese e deterioramenti

## Summary
Article 748 of the Italian Civil Code governs the treatment of improvements, extraordinary expenses, and deterioration when a donee (donatario) collates an asset (typically an immovable) into the hereditary estate for division. It sets rules for deducting the value of improvements up to their worth at the time of the succession opening, reimbursing extraordinary conservation expenses not caused by the donee’s fault, and holding the donee liable for culpable deterioration. The article also grants the co‑heir who collates an immovable in kind a right to retain possession until reimbursement for due expenses and improvements is made.

## Key Details
- **Improvements**: The donee is entitled to a deduction for improvements made to the property, limited to their value at the time of the succession opening ([[art456]], [[art1150]]).
- **Extraordinary expenses**: Expenses borne by the donee for the conservation of the property, not caused by the donee’s fault, must also be reimbursed ([[art1150]], [[art1152]]).
- **Deterioration**: The donee is liable for deterioration of the property caused by their own fault, reducing the immovable’s value.
- **Right of retention**: A co‑heir who collates an immovable in kind (see [[art746]]) may retain possession until actually reimbursed for the amounts due for expenses and improvements ([[art749]], [[art975]], [[art985]], [[art1152]], [[art2040]]).
- The article is located in **Book II – Successions**, Title IV (Division), Chapter II (Collation) of the Codice Civile.
