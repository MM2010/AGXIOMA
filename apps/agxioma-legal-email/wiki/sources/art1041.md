# Art. 1041 Codice Civile - Letto dell'acquedotto

## Summary
This article grants the owner of the servient land (fondo servente) the right to permanently fix the bed of a coactive aqueduct by placing stable benchmarks or thresholds. If the owner does not exercise this right at the time the aqueduct is granted, he must bear half of the costs incurred for setting the bed.

## Key Details
- The owner of the servient land has always the faculty to have the aqueduct bed determined stably.
- The determination is done by apposition of capisaldi (benchmarks) or soglie (thresholds) that must be referred to fixed points.
- If the owner does not use this faculty at the time of the aqueduct’s concession, he is obliged to pay half of the necessary expenses.
- The article belongs to Book III (Property), Title VI (Predial Servitudes), Chapter II (Coactive Servitudes), Section I (Coactive Aqueduct and Drainage).
- It reflects a balance between the interests of the dominant and servient estates in the context of compulsory servitudes.
