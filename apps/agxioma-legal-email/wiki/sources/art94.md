# Art. 94 codice penale - Ubriachezza abituale

## Summary
This article of the Italian Penal Code establishes habitual drunkenness (ubriachezza abituale) as an aggravating circumstance for any crime committed while in that state. Habitual drunkenness is defined as a condition where the person is addicted to alcohol use and frequently found intoxicated. The same penalty increase applies when the crime is committed under the influence of narcotic substances by a person addicted to their use.

## Key Details
- The penalty for a crime committed in a state of drunkenness is increased if the drunkenness is habitual (first paragraph).
- Habitual drunkenness is legally defined as: being addicted to the use of alcoholic beverages and frequently in a state of intoxication.
- The aggravating rule applies identically to crimes committed under the influence of narcotic substances by persons addicted to their use (third paragraph, referencing Law 689/1975 amendment).
- This article is part of Title IV ("Del reo e della persona offesa dal reato"), Chapter I ("Della imputabilità") of the Penal Code.
- The increase applies to any type of crime, not only those specifically related to intoxication.
- The article does not specify the amount of penalty increase; it is determined by the judge within the general limits of the aggravating circumstances.
