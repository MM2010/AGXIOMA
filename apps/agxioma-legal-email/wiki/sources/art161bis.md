# Art. 161 bis codice penale - Cessazione del corso della prescrizione

## Summary
Art. 161 bis c.p. regulates the definitive cessation of the statute of limitations for a crime. The limitation period terminates irrevocably upon the pronouncement of a first-instance judgment. However, if an annulment causes the proceedings to regress to the first instance or an earlier phase, the limitation period resumes its course from the date of the definitive annulment decision.

## Key Details
- The course of prescription ceases definitively with the pronouncement of the first-instance sentence.
- Upon annulment that causes regression of the proceedings to the first degree or an earlier phase, prescription resumes from the date of the definitive annulment.
- This article is part of Title VI (Extinction of the Crime and Penalty), Capo I (Extinction of the Crime).
- Provides a rule of final cessation, unlike the suspension or interruption rules found in other articles.
- The provision balances finality of criminal proceedings with the possibility of procedural reversals after annulment.
