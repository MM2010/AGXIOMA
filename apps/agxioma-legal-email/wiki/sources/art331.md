# Art. 331 codice penale - Interruzione d'un servizio pubblico o di pubblica necessità

## Summary
Art. 331 c.p. punishes anyone who, operating a public service or a service of public necessity, interrupts the service or suspends work in its establishments, offices, or undertakings in a manner that disturbs the regularity of the service. The basic penalty is imprisonment from six months to one year and a fine of at least €516. Leaders, promoters, or organizers face aggravated penalties of three to seven years’ imprisonment and a fine of at least €3,098. The article also incorporates the last paragraph of the preceding article (Art. 330 c.p.), which typically regulates judicial effects such as the restitution of property or other consequences of the interruption.

## Key Details
- **Article**: Art. 331 c.p. – “Interruzione d'un servizio pubblico o di pubblica necessità” (Interruption of a public service or service of public necessity).
- **Location**: Book II (Delitti in particolare), Title II (Delitti contro la pubblica amministrazione), Chapter I (Dei delitti dei pubblici ufficiali contro la pubblica amministrazione).
- **Subject**: Applies to private persons (or entities) that carry out public‑service or public‑necessity enterprises (not only public officials).
- **Actus reus**: Interruption of the service or suspension of work in the enterprise’s facilities, thereby disturbing the regular performance of the service.
- **Basic penalty**: Reclusione da sei mesi a un anno e multa non inferiore a euro 516.
- **Aggravated penalty (leaders/organizers)**: Reclusione da tre a sette anni e multa non inferiore a euro 3.098.
- **Cross‑reference**: Incorporation of the last paragraph of Art. 330 c.p. (which deals with the revocation or suspension of public employment or the ordering of restitution).
- **Ratio**: Protects the continuity and regularity of essential public services against disruptive conduct by the very entities that operate them.
- **Distinction**: Art. 330 c.p. sanctions public officials who interrupt a service; Art. 331 c.p. targets private operators of such services.
