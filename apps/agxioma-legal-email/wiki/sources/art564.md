# Art. 564 c.p. - Incesto

## Summary
This article of the Italian Penal Code (Book II, Title XI, Chapter II) criminalises incestuous acts between direct ancestors/descendants, in-laws in the direct line, or siblings, provided the act causes public scandal. It establishes a base punishment of 1–5 years of imprisonment, with an aggravating circumstance (the precise condition is truncated in the source) raising the penalty to 2–8 years.

## Key Details
- **Required element:** The incest must be committed “in modo che ne derivi pubblico scandalo” (so as to cause public scandal).
- **Protected relationships:** ascendants/descendants ([[art75]]), in-laws in the direct line ([[art78]]), and siblings.
- **Base penalty:** reclusione da 1 a 5 anni.
- **Aggravated penalty:** reclusione da 2 a 8 anni in the case of … (the source snippet is incomplete; the full article typically aggravates when the offence is committed by an adult with a minor or by an ascendant with a descendant).
- **Location in the Code:** Book II, Title XI (Crimes against the family), Chapter II (Crimes against family morality).
