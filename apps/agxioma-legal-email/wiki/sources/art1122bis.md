# Art. 1122 bis c.c. - Impianti non centralizzati di ricezione radiotelevisiva e di produzione di energia da fonti rinnovabili

## Summary

This article of the Italian Civil Code regulates the installation of non‑centralized systems for radio/television reception (including satellite and cable) and for the production of energy from renewable sources (e.g., photovoltaic panels) in condominium buildings. It balances the individual owner’s right to install such systems with the need to protect common parts, the architectural decor, and the interests of other condominium owners. The condominium assembly may impose alternative execution methods or require guarantees for potential damages.

## Key Details

- Installations must cause the least possible prejudice to common parts and to individual units, while preserving the building’s architectural decor (*decoro architettonico*).
- Renewable energy systems are allowed on the roof terrace (*lastrico solare*), on any other suitable common surface, and on the individual owner’s private property.
- If modifications to common parts are necessary, the interested party must notify the administrator, specifying the content and execution methods.
- The assembly, with the qualified majority set out in the fifth paragraph of [[art1136]], may prescribe alternative execution methods or impose precautions for stability, safety, and architectural decor.
- Upon request of interested parties, the assembly shall allocate the use of the roof terrace and other common surfaces, safeguarding existing uses provided for in the condominium regulations or in place.
- The assembly may also require the interested party to provide a suitable guarantee for potential damages.
- Access to individual units must be granted when needed for designing and carrying out the works.
- Systems intended solely for single residential units are not subject to assembly authorization, though they must still comply with the other provisions of the article.
