# Art. 1094 codice civile - Servitù attiva degli scoli

## Summary
Art. 1094 of the Italian Civil Code governs the **active easement of drainage waters** (*servitù attiva degli scoli*). It provides that the owner of a lower or receiving land may acquire an easement over the waters that drain naturally or artificially from the higher land, for the purpose of preventing their diversion. The norm is located in Book Third (Property), Title VI (Predial Servitudes), Chapter VIII (Certain Servitudes concerning Waters), Section II (Servitude of Drains and Residual Waters). It establishes a right that allows the dominant land to permanently benefit from the flow of waste or excess water, subject to the requirements of title, usucapion, or destination of the father of the family.

## Key Details
- **Codice Civile**: Libro Terzo, Titolo VI, Capo VIII, Sezione II.
- The servitude is *active*: the dominant land has the right to receive the waters and may prohibit the servient owner from diverting them.
- It applies to waters that are "colaticce" (seepage, waste, or excess waters) from another's land.
- The right is established in favor of the land that receives the waters (fondo dominante), not the one that sends them.
- This provision complements the passive duty of lower lands to receive waters (Art. 913) and the general regulation of water easements.
- The easement can arise by *title* (contract, will), *usucapione* (20‑year possession), or *destinazione del padre di famiglia* (apparent and continuous use by the common owner).
- To prevent diversion, the dominant owner can exercise legal actions (*actio confessoria servitutis*).
- The article is part of a series that also includes servitudes for residual waters (Section II) and for the aqueduct (Section III).
