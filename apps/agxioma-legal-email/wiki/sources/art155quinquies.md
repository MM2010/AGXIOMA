# Art. 155 quinquies codice civile - Disposizioni in favore dei figli maggiorenni

## Summary
Article 155 quinquies of the Italian Civil Code, introduced by a previous reform, governed the obligation of a parent (or both parents) to pay a periodic allowance to adult children who were not economically independent, in the context of separation or divorce. This provision was completely abrogated by Article 106(1)(a) of D.Lgs. 28 December 2013, n. 154, which reformed the law on parental responsibility and child support, absorbing the protection of adult non‑self‑sufficient children into the general rules on child support (Articles 337 septies and following). The article also special‑treated adult children with severe disabilities, applying to them the full safeguards granted to minors.

## Key Details
- **Status:** Abrogated (repealed) as of 28 December 2013 by D.Lgs. n. 154/2013.
- **Original scope:** Allowed a judge, after evaluating circumstances, to order a periodic allowance for adult children lacking economic independence.
- **Direct payment:** Unless the judge decided otherwise, the allowance was to be paid directly to the entitled child.
- **Disabled children:** For adult children with severe disabilities (as per Law 104/1992, art. 3(3)), the same full protections applicable to minor children applied.
- **Context:** Part of the original regulation of separation and divorce (before the 2013 reform merged many provisions into new rules on parental responsibility).
- **Replaced by:** Current rules in Articles 337 septies ff. cc., which provide for child support for both minor and adult non‑self‑sufficient children.
