# Art. 551 codice penale - Causa di onore

## Summary
Article 551 of the Italian Penal Code provided a mitigating circumstance for certain crimes against the integrity and health of the race (stirpe) — specifically those listed in Articles 545–550 — when committed to save one's own honor or that of a close relative. The article was abrogated by Law 22 May 1978, n. 194, which repealed the entire Title X (Dei delitti contro la integrità e la sanità della stirpe) of the Penal Code.

## Key Details
- Article 551 belonged to Title X of Book II of the Italian Penal Code (abrogated).
- It reduced the penalties for the crimes under [[art545]] (now abrogated), [[art546]], [[art547]], [[art548]], [[art549]], and [[art550]] by one-half to two-thirds.
- The mitigating factor required that the act be committed to save the honor of the perpetrator or a close relative (causa di onore).
- The entire Title X was abrogated by art. 22, L. 22 May 1978, n. 194 (Law on the voluntary termination of pregnancy).
- The article is no longer in force and is of historical and doctrinal interest only.
- The source content originates from Brocardi.it, an Italian legal database.
