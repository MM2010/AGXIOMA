# Art. 287 codice penale - Usurpazione di potere politico o di comando militare

## Summary
This article, located in Book II, Title I (crimes against the internal personality of the state) of the Italian Penal Code, criminalizes the usurpation of political power or the indebited exercise thereof, as well as the unauthorized assumption of high military command. Penalties range from 6 to 15 years of imprisonment for peacetime violations, escalating to life imprisonment (ergastolo) during war, and even the death penalty (now de facto abolished) if the act compromises military operations. It serves to protect the constitutional order and the legitimate chain of command.

## Key Details
- **Offence**: Usurpation of political power (any form) or persisting in its indebited exercise.
- **Military command**: Unauthorized assumption of a "high military command" – no specific rank is defined, interpreted as top-level leadership (e.g., general, admiral).
- **Punishment (peacetime)**: Reclusione da 6 a 15 anni.
- **Punishment (wartime)**: Ergastolo (life imprisonment).
- **Aggravated wartime variant**: If the usurpation compromises the outcome of military operations, the punishment is ergastolo (originally the death penalty, as indicated by the bracketed `[la morte]` in the source text, but capital punishment for military crimes was formally abolished with Constitutional amendments and Law 589/1994).
- **Reference to war**: The article explicitly cross-references [[art310]] (definition of "tempo di guerra").
- **Relationship with other offences**: Part of Chapter II (crimes against the internal personality of the state), complementary to articles on subversion, coup d'état, and insurrection.
- **Scope**: Applies to both civilians and military personnel; the political usurpation covers de facto exercise of power without legal title.
- **Limitation**: The military command must be "high" – lower command misappropriation falls under other provisions (e.g., military penal code).
