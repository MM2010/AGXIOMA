# Art. 719 Penal Code – Aggravating Circumstances (Gambling)

## Summary
Article 719 of the Italian Penal Code (R.D. 1930) provides aggravating circumstances for the basic illegal-gambling offense defined in the preceding article (Art. 718). When any of the listed conditions are met, the penalty is doubled. The article is part of the third book (contravvenzioni) dealing with police contraventions, specifically those concerning public morality and social administrative police.

## Key Details
- The base penalty for illegal gambling (Art. 718) is doubled under Art. 719.
- Four alternative aggravating circumstances are listed:
  1. The offender established or kept a gambling house (as defined in Art. 721).
  2. The offense was committed in a public establishment (e.g., bar, restaurant, club).
  3. The stakes involved are substantial ("poste rilevanti").
  4. Among the participants there are persons under eighteen years of age (Art. 723).
- The article belongs to Section I (Contravvenzioni concerning the police of customs) of Chapter II (Contravvenzioni concerning social administrative police), Title I, Book III of the Penal Code.
- The aggravated offense remains a contravention (contravvenzione), not a delitto.
