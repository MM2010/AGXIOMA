# Art. 1038 codice civile – Indennità per l’imposizione della servitù

## Summary
This article ([[art1038]]) sets the compensation rules for the compulsory imposition of an aqueduct or drainage easement under Italian law. Before building the aqueduct across another’s land, the person exercising the easement must pay the full estimated value of the occupied land (without deducting taxes or charges) plus an indemnity for damages, including those caused by splitting the land into two or more parts. A reduced indemnity (half the land value) applies to land used only for depositing extracted materials or for discharge.

## Key Details
- The indemnity is calculated on the full market value of the occupied land; taxes and other burdens attached to the land are not deducted.
- In addition to land value, compensation covers all damages, including those resulting from the severance of the land into two or more parts or other deterioration.
- For land used exclusively for storing extracted materials or for waste discharge, the indemnity is only half the land value (still without deduction of taxes).
- The owner of the servient land may plant vegetation and remove/transport accumulated materials on such storage/discharge land, provided it does not harm the aqueduct, its maintenance, or its discharge.
- These provisions are part of the broader framework for compulsory easements ([[art1032]]) and are linked to subsequent articles ([[art1039]]) for further details.
