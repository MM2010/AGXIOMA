# Art. 600 sexies codice penale - Circostanze aggravanti ed attenuanti

## Summary
Art. 600 sexies c.p. (abrogated by L. 1 October 2012, n. 172) established a set of aggravating and mitigating circumstances applicable to several offenses against minors: [[art600-cp]], [[art600bis-cp]], [[art600ter-cp]], [[art600quater-cp]], [[art600quinquies-cp]], [[art600septies-cp]], [[art600octies-cp]], [[art601-cp]], [[art602-cp]], and [[art416-cp]] (sesto comma). Aggravations were based on the victim’s age, the perpetrator’s relationship with the minor, use of violence or threat, and the minor’s state of mental infirmity. Mitigations rewarded concrete efforts to restore the minor’s autonomy or to cooperate with authorities in securing evidence and apprehending offenders. A special rule prevented certain mitigating circumstances from being considered equivalent or prevalent over the aggravations, ensuring that any reduction would be applied on the increased penalty.

## Key Details
- **Abrogation**: Repealed by L. 172/2012; its provisions are no longer in force.
- **Aggravating circumstances**:
  - Penalty increased by one-third to one-half if the victim was under 14 years of age (for offenses under [[art600bis-cp]] first comma, [[art600ter-cp]] first comma, [[art600quinquies-cp]], as well as [[art600-cp]], [[art601-cp]], [[art602-cp]]).
  - Penalty increased by one-half to two-thirds if the victim was a minor and the perpetrator was an ancestor, adoptive parent, spouse or cohabitant of such, certain relatives, guardian, person entrusted with care/education/supervision, or a public official acting in their duties, or if the minor suffered from mental infirmity (natural or induced).
  - Separate aggravation (increase unspecified) for using violence or threat in offenses under [[art600bis-cp]] first comma and [[art600ter-cp]].
- **Mitigating circumstances**:
  - Reduction by one-third to one-half for those who concretely act to help the minor (under 18) regain autonomy and liberty (applicable to [[art600bis-cp]], [[art600ter-cp]], [[art600-cp]], [[art601-cp]], [[art602-cp]]).
  - Reduction up to one-half for the defendant who works to prevent further consequences of the criminal activity and concretely assists police or judicial authority in gathering decisive evidence or identifying/capturing perpetrators or seizing significant resources (applicable to the full list of articles including [[art416-cp]] sesto comma).
- **Special rule on concurring circumstances**: Mitigating circumstances other than the minority age reduction ([[art98-cp]]) could not be held equivalent or prevalent over the first two aggravations; any reduction would be applied on the penalty already increased by those aggravations.
- **Historical significance**: The article structured the penalty enhancements and reductions for a core set of offenses against minors, reflecting legislative policy to both punish severely and incentivize rescue and cooperation.
