# Art. 844 cod. civ. – Immissioni

## Summary
Art. 844 c.c. regulates interferences (immissioni) — such as smoke, heat, fumes, noise, vibrations — between neighboring properties. The owner of a plot cannot prevent these propagations unless they exceed *normal tolerability* (normale tollerabilità), considering the condition of the place. When applying the rule, the court must balance production needs with property rights, and may take into account the priority of a given use. This provision is central to Italian nuisance law and property relations.

## Key Details
- Applies to immissions of fumo, calore, esalazioni, rumori, scuotimenti and similar propagations from the neighbour’s fund.
- The test is whether the interference surpasses **normal tollerability** (normale tollerabilità), also having regard to the condition of the places.
- The judge must **contemperate** (balance) production exigencies with the reasons of property.
- Courts may consider the **priority of a particular use** (e.g., industrial facility built before a residential area).
- The threshold of normal tollerability is objective and fact‑sensitive, not merely subjective sensitivity.
- Does not require proof of actual damage; the prohibition is preventive.
- The rule is mandatory — parties cannot contractually waive the protection beyond statutory limits.
- Jurisprudence has extended the concept to electromagnetic fields, odors, and even psychological disturbance.
