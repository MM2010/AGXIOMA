# Art. 373 codice penale - Falsa perizia o interpretazione

## Summary
Art. 373 of the Italian Penal Code criminalizes false expert opinions or interpretations (falsa perizia o interpretazione) given by a court-appointed expert or interpreter. The provision applies the same penalties as false testimony under [[art372-cp]] and mandates additional consequences including interdiction from public office and from the profession or art. It is a crime against the administration of justice, aimed at protecting the truthfulness of judicial proceedings.

## Key Details
- **Actus reus**: The perito (expert) or interprete (interpreter), nominated by the judicial authority (artt. 61, 122-123, 220, 221, 224, 143 c.p.p.), gives mendacious opinions or interpretations, or affirms facts not corresponding to the truth.
- **Penalty**: The same punishments as established in the preceding article ([[art372-cp]]), i.e., imprisonment from 2 to 6 years.
- **Mandatory additional consequences**: The conviction entails:
  - Interdiction from public offices ([[art28-cp]]).
  - Interdiction from the profession or art ([[art30-cp]]).
- **Location in the Code**: Libro II, Titolo III (Delitti contro l'amministrazione della giustizia), Capo I (Dei delitti contro l'attività giudiziaria).
- **Related procedural norms**: artt. 198, 476 c.p.p. and artt. 375-377, 384 c.p.
- **Nature**: The crime is consummated upon the mendacious statement, regardless of its actual influence on the proceeding; it is a formal offense against the administration of justice.
- **Retraction**: Possible application of the special cause of non-punishment under [[art384-cp]] (if the false statement is retracted before the closure of the deliberation).
