# Art. 518 quinquies codice penale - Impiego di beni culturali provenienti da delitto

## Summary
This article punishes the use of cultural goods derived from a crime in economic or financial activities, outside the cases of complicity in the original crime and outside the specific cases covered by [[art518quater]] (Ricettazione di beni culturali) and [[art518sexies]] (Riciclaggio di beni culturali). The penalty is imprisonment from five to thirteen years and a fine from €6,000 to €30,000. Importantly, the provision applies even when the author of the underlying crime is not imputable, not punishable, or when a condition of procedibility for that crime is lacking. This ensures that the employment of tainted cultural assets is sanctioned regardless of the fate of the original offender.

## Key Details
- Punishes the employment of cultural property coming from any crime (delitto) in economic or financial activities.
- Applies only when the defendant is not complicit in the original crime and the conduct does not fall under [[art518quater]] (receiving) or [[art518sexies]] (money laundering).
- Penalty: reclusione da 5 a 13 anni e multa da €6.000 a €30.000.
- The provision also applies if the original offender is not imputable (e.g., mental incapacity) or not punishable (e.g., immunity) or if the prerequisite for prosecution of that crime is missing.
- Belongs to **Titolo VIII-bis** ("Dei delitti contro il patrimonio culturale") of the Penal Code.
