# Art. 590 quinquies codice penale - Definizione di strade urbane e extraurbane

## Summary
This article (introduced by the 2016 road homicide reform) provides a binding definition of "urban centre roads" and "extra‑urban roads" for the exclusive purposes of articles 589 bis (road homicide) and 590 bis (road personal injuries) of the Italian Penal Code. It incorporates by reference the classification of roads set out in Article 2, paragraph 2, of Legislative Decree 30 April 1992, n. 285 (New Highway Code). The distinction is crucial because the applicable penalties and aggravating circumstances under those offences depend on whether the conduct occurred on an extra‑urban road (types A, B, C) or on a road within an urban centre (types D, E, F, F‑bis).

## Key Details
- **Purpose**: resolves interpretative doubts by linking penal provisions directly to the Highway Code classification.
- **Extra‑urban roads**: correspond to letters A (motorways), B (principal arterial roads), and C (secondary arterial roads) under art. 2, comma 2, D.Lgs. 285/1992.
- **Urban centre roads**: correspond to letters D (urban arterial roads), E (urban neighbourhood roads), F (local roads), and F‑bis (itineraries for pedestrians and cyclists) of the same classification.
- **Scope limited**: the definitions apply *only* to articles 589 bis and 590 bis, not to other penal or civil contexts.
- **Integral part of the reform**: introduced by Law 41/2016 (the so‑called "omicidio stradale" law) to ensure uniform application of the new offences.
- **No direct penalty**: this article is purely definitional; it does not establish any separate offence or sanction.
