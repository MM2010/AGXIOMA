# Art. 57 codice penale - Reati commessi col mezzo della stampa periodica

## Summary
Article 57 of the Italian Penal Code establishes the criminal liability of the director or vice-director of a periodical who, by omitting the necessary control over the content of the publication, fails to prevent crimes from being committed through the press. When the author of the publication is not held responsible (or absent a concurrence of parties), the director or vice-director is punished for negligence (colpa) if a crime actually occurs, with the penalty prescribed for that crime reduced by up to one third.

## Key Details
- Article 57 applies specifically to crimes committed through periodic press (newspapers, magazines, etc.).
- Liability arises only when the author of the publication is not responsible and there is no concurrence of parties.
- The director or vice-director must omit the control necessary to prevent the commission of crimes through the periodical.
- Punishment is imposed *a titolo di colpa* (on the basis of negligence), not intent.
- The penalty is the same as that established for the crime committed, diminished by an amount not exceeding one third (per [[art36]]).
- The provision is located in Book I, Title III (Del reato), Chapter I (Del reato consumato e tentato) of the Italian Penal Code.
- References multiple other articles: [[art528]], [[art565]], [[art596bis]], [[art683]], [[art684]], [[art685]].
