# Art. 575 codice penale - Omicidio

## Summary
Art. 575 of the Italian Penal Code defines the basic crime of voluntary homicide (*omicidio doloso*). It punishes anyone who causes the death of a person with imprisonment for no less than twenty‑one years. This article serves as the central norm for unlawful killings and is complemented by aggravating circumstances (e.g., [[art576-cp]], [[art577-cp]]) and by related provisions on premeditated homicide, infanticide, and other life‑endangering offenses.

## Key Details
- **Crime**: Causing the death of a human being (*cagiona la morte di un uomo*).
- **Minimum penalty**: Imprisonment for no less than 21 years (reclusione non inferiore ad anni ventuno).
- **Nature**: General provision for voluntary homicide (dolo generico).
- **Cross‑references within the article**: Explicit mentions of articles 276, 295, 579 (infanticide) and the navigation code (c. nav. 1150) as related or special norms.
- **Systematic position**: Part of Book II, Title XII (crimes against the person), Chapter I (crimes against life and personal safety).
- **Interpretation**: The expression "morte di un uomo" includes any individual from birth until death; self‑defense or other justifications are handled under separate articles (e.g., [[art52-cp]]).
- **Penalty floor**: 21 years is mandatory; courts may impose higher terms up to life imprisonment under aggravating circumstances.
- **Relationship with other offenses**: When the homicide is committed with particular cruelty, against certain persons, or with premeditation, the punishment is increased (see [[art576-cp]]).
