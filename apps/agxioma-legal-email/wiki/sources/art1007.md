# Art. 1007 codice civile - Rovina parziale di edificio accessorio

## Summary
Art. 1007 c.c. extends the rules of the two preceding articles (Art. 1005 and 1006) to the case where, due to age or fortuitous event, only part of the building that constitutes a necessary accessory of the land subject to usufruct collapses. It confirms that the usufructuary's obligations to repair or reconstruct (as provided in the earlier articles) apply proportionally to partial ruin of such accessory buildings, preserving the integrity of the usufructuary's duties.

## Key Details
- Part of Book III, Title V, Chapter I, Section III of the Italian Civil Code (obligations arising from usufruct).
- Reference article: **Art. 1007**. 
- Applies when a building which is a **necessary accessory** of the land under usufruct partially collapses.
- Causes: **vetustà** (age/decay) or **caso fortuito** (fortuitous event/force majeure).
- Extends the legal regime of **Art. 1005** and **Art. 1006** (which cover total ruin or destruction) to partial ruin.
- The usufructuary remains responsible for reconstruction/repair according to the general principles of usufruct obligations, proportionally to the partial damage.
- Does not create new obligations but clarifies the continuity of existing duties for partial collapses.
- No explicit penalties; enforcement follows the general rules of the civil code.
