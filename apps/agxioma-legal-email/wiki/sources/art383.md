# Art. 383 codice penale - Interdizione dai pubblici uffici

## Summary
The Article 383 of the Italian Penal Code (Codice Penale) provides that a conviction for the crimes set forth in Articles 380, 381 (first part), and 382 automatically entails the accessory penalty of interdiction from public offices (interdizione dai pubblici uffici) as defined in Article 28 of the Penal Code. This supplemental sanction is directly linked to certain serious offences against judicial activity, reinforcing the protective function of the administration of justice.

## Key Details
- Applies to convictions under Articles 380, 381 (first part), and 382 of the Penal Code.
- The penalty imposed is the interdiction from public offices (art. 28 cp), which prohibits the convicted person from holding any public office, elective or appointive, and from exercising related rights.
- Belongs to Book II, Title III (crimes against the administration of justice), Chapter I (crimes against judicial activity).
- The automatic nature of the interdiction means it does not require a separate judicial assessment; it follows directly upon conviction for the listed crimes.
- Article 380 deals with false testimony (falsa testimonianza) before the judicial authority; Article 381 (first part) deals with false information to the public prosecutor (false informazioni al pubblico ministero); Article 382 deals with false declaration or certification to a notary or public official (falsa dichiarazione o attestazione al notaio o a pubblico ufficiale).
