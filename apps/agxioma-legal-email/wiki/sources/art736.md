# Art. 736 codice civile - Consegna dei documenti

## Summary
Art. 736 c.c. governs the delivery and custody of documents after the division of an inheritance. It ensures that each co‑heir receives the documents concerning the property specifically assigned to them, while documents relating to property that has been divided (or common to the entire estate) are entrusted to the heir with the largest share or, in equal shares, to a person chosen by all interested parties. If no agreement is reached on the custodian, the court of the place where the succession was opened appoints one.

## Key Details
- After division, documents pertaining exclusively to the property or rights assigned to a particular co‑heir must be delivered to that heir. (first paragraph)
- If the divided property is not equally split, the documents remain with the heir who received the largest share; that heir is obliged to show them to the other co‑heirs upon request. (second paragraph)
- When property is divided into equal shares, or when documents concern the entire inheritance, they are delivered to a person chosen by all interested parties. (third paragraph)
- The chosen custodian must communicate the documents to any co‑heir who asks for them. (third paragraph)
- In case of disagreement on the selection of the custodian, the tribunal of the place where the succession was opened (see [[art456]]) decides by decree after hearing the interested parties. (third paragraph)
- The article cross‑references [[art2646]] (publicity of documents) and [[art456]] (opening of succession).
