# Art. 845 codice civile - Regole particolari per scopi di pubblico interesse

## Summary
Article 845 of the Italian Civil Code is a general provision within the section on land ownership (proprietà fondiaria). It states that land ownership is subject to special rules aimed at achieving public interest goals, as established by special laws and by the subsequent sections of the Civil Code itself. This article serves as a gateway to numerous specific limitations and obligations on landowners for the benefit of the community.

## Key Details
- Located in Book III (Della proprietà), Title II (Della proprietà), Chapter II (Della proprietà fondiaria), Section I (Disposizioni generali).
- The article does not itself impose specific restrictions; it references "special laws" and the following sections of the code.
- It reflects the principle that private property rights can be limited for public interest purposes, consistent with constitutional principles (cf. [[art42-cost]]).
- The "regole particolari" include, for example, rules on expropriation, building restrictions, water rights, and environmental protection.
- It is a foundational norm for interpreting subsequent articles within the same chapter and for applying sector-specific legislation.
