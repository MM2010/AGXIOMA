# Art. 1078 codice civile - Servitù costituite a favore del fondo enfiteutico, [dotale] o in usufrutto

## Summary
Article 1078 of the Italian Civil Code establishes that servitudes (predial easements) created by the **enfiteuta** (emphyteuta) in favor of the emphyteutic property survive the extinction of the emphyteusis. The same principle applies to servitudes created by the **usufructuary** in favor of the property subject to usufruct, and, under the former dotal regime, to servitudes constituted by the husband for the dotal fund. The rule ensures that the benefit of such servitudes remains attached to the dominant tenement regardless of the termination of the limited real right that created them.

## Key Details
- Article belongs to **Book III – Property**, Title VI – Predial servitudes, Chapter VI – Extinction of servitudes.
- The rule is an exception to the general principle that servitudes created by a limited right holder normally extinguish with that right (cf. [[art1028-cc]] for usufruct).
- References [[art959-cc]] which defines the rights of the enfiteuta to constitute servitudes.
- The provision regarding the **dotal fund** (dotale) is now essentially obsolete, as the dotal system was abolished by Law 151/1975 (family law reform).
- The servitude must have been **constituted in favor** of the dominant tenement (fondo enfiteutico, fondo in usufrutto, or fondo dotale), not merely by the holder.
- The survival applies **only to servitudes** (not to other encumbrances or rights).
- The rule prevents the dominant tenement from losing an easement solely because the personal right (emphyteusis, usufruct) that created it has terminated.
