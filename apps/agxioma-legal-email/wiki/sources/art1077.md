# Art. 1077 codice civile – Servitù costituite sul fondo enfiteutico

## Summary
The article regulates the fate of servitudes (servitù) created by the emphyteuta ([[art959]]) on the emphyteutic land. It establishes that such servitudes automatically extinguish when the emphyteusis itself ends, whether by expiration of the term, prescription, or devolution. The rule ensures that the rights burdening the land revert to its full owner once the limited real right of emphyteusis is dissolved.

## Key Details
- Located in Book III, Title VI, Chapter VI of the Civil Code (“Dell’estinzione delle servitù”).
- Applies to servitudes constituted exclusively by the emphyteuta (not by the direct owner or other parties).
- Extinction occurs in three scenarios: decorso del termine (term expiry), prescrizione (prescription), devoluzione (forfeiture or reversion).
- After extinction of the emphyteusis, the servitudes cease *ipso iure* without need for further acts.
- The rule does not affect servitudes already existing on the land before the emphyteusis was created.
- Reflects the temporary and derivative nature of the emphyteuta’s powers over the fund.
