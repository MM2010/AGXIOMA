# Art. 957 codice civile - Disposizioni inderogabili

## Summary
Article 957 of the Italian Civil Code, placed in Book III (Property), Title IV (Emphyteusis), establishes that emphyteusis is governed by the following articles unless the title (contract or deed) provides otherwise. However, the title cannot derogate from the mandatory provisions listed in the second paragraph, namely articles 958 (second paragraph), 961 (second paragraph), 962, 965, 968, 971, and 973. This article thus distinguishes between default rules and inderogable core protections for emphyteusis.

## Key Details
- Identifies the set of rules applicable to emphyteusis (articles 958–973), with the possibility for the title to deviate unless otherwise stated.
- Lists the articles that are completely inderogable: [[art958]] (second paragraph), [[art961]] (second paragraph), [[art962]], [[art965]], [[art968]], [[art971]], [[art973]].
- The inderogable provisions concern essential aspects such as the minimum duration, the grantor's rights, the emphyteuta's obligations, and termination.
- Article 957 itself does not contain substantive rules but acts as a gatekeeper for the entire Title IV regime.
- Derived from the Italian Civil Code (Codice Civile).
