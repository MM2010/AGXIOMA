# Art. 917 codice civile - Spese per la riparazione, costruzione o rimozione

## Summary
Art. 917 of the Italian Civil Code governs the allocation of expenses for the maintenance, construction, or removal of riverbanks, embankments, and obstructions in watercourses. It establishes a general rule of proportional contribution based on the benefit each landowner receives, but shifts the entire cost (plus damages) to the negligent owner whose fault caused the destruction, alteration, or obstruction. The provision enforces equitable cost‑sharing among co‑interested proprietaries while preserving individual liability for misconduct.

## Key Details
- Applies to all proprietors who derive utility from the conservation or construction of banks and embankments, or the removal of obstructions in water flows.
- Expenses are shared proportionally to the advantage each owner obtains from the work (proportional contribution rule).
- If the destruction of embankments, diversion of water, or obstruction of watercourses is caused by the fault of a specific owner, that owner bears the entire cost of conservation, construction, or repair.
- In addition to bearing the full expense, the negligent owner is liable for damages caused to others (art. 2043 e ss. c.c.).
- The article forms part of the Italian Civil Code (Book III, Title II, Chapter II, Section IX – Waters).
- It balances collective interest in watercourse maintenance with individual accountability for fault‑based harm.
