# Art. 155 quater codice civile - Assegnazione della casa familiare e prescrizioni in tema di residenza

## Summary
This former article of the Italian Civil Code (abrogated by D.lgs. 28 December 2013, n. 154) governed the assignment of the family home in cases of marital separation or divorce. It prescribed that the judge must primarily consider the children’s interest when assigning the home, and that the assigned right ceases if the beneficiary no longer resides there, cohabits with a new partner, or remarries. The provision also allowed for the transcription (registration) of the assignment or revocation order, making it enforceable against third parties under [[art2643]]. Additionally, it permitted a spouse to request a redefinition of agreements if the other spouse changed residence or domicile in a manner interfering with child custody arrangements.

## Key Details
- **Abrogated**: Repealed by Legislative Decree 28 December 2013, n. 154 (reform of filiation and family law).
- **Primary criterion**: Assignment of the family home must prioritize the best interest of the children.
- **Economic adjustment**: The judge considers the assignment when regulating economic relations between parents, including any property title.
- **Cessation of right**: The right to occupy the family home ends if the assignee (a) no longer habitually resides there, (b) cohabits *more uxorio* (as a couple), or (c) remarries.
- **Transcription**: Both the assignment and revocation orders are subject to transcription (registration) and are opposable to third parties under art. 2643 of the Civil Code.
- **Residence change**: If one spouse changes residence or domicile, the other spouse may seek revision of custody agreements (including economic terms) if the change interferes with custody arrangements.
- **Context**: Belongs to Book I, Title VI, Chapter V of the Civil Code (“Dissolution of marriage and separation of spouses”).
