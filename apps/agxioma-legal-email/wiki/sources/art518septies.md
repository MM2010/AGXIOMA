# Art. 518 septies codice penale - Autoriciclaggio di beni culturali

## Summary
**Art. 518 septies CP** (introduced by the 2022 reform on cultural heritage crimes) punishes the self-laundering of cultural assets. The article criminalises anyone who, after committing or participating in a non‑negligent offence, employs, substitutes or transfers cultural goods derived from that offence in economic, financial, entrepreneurial or speculative activities in a way that concretely hinders the identification of their criminal origin. The penalty is imprisonment from 3 to 10 years and a fine of €6,000 to €30,000; if the predicate offence carries a maximum imprisonment of less than 5 years, the penalty is reduced to 2–5 years and a fine of €3,000–€15,000. Conduct limited to mere personal use or enjoyment is exempt from punishment. The third paragraph of [[art518quater]] applies to confiscation and other measures.

## Key Details
- **Scope**: applies to any non‑negligent predicate offence generating cultural assets (i.e., goods of artistic, historical, archaeological or ethno‑anthropological interest)
- **Conduct**: *impiegare* (employ), *sostituire* (substitute), *trasferire* (transfer) the cultural goods in activities of an economic, financial, entrepreneurial or speculative nature
- **Obstruction requirement**: the conduct must concretely hinder the tracing of the criminal provenance of the assets
- **Penalty base**: reclusione da 3 a 10 anni + multa da €6.000 a €30.000
- **Reduced penalty**: if the predicate offence carries a maximum prison term < 5 years, the penalty drops to 2‑5 years + multa da €3.000 a €15.000
- **Personal use exemption**: conduct aimed purely at personal utilisation or enjoyment is not punishable (outside the first two paragraphs)
- **Relation to art. 518 quater**: the third comma of [[art518quater]] (likely dealing with confiscation or aggravating circumstances) applies by reference
- **Effective for predicate offences committed after the entry into force of the 2022 reform** (D.Lgs. 42/2022?)
- Part of **Titolo VIII‑bis CP** – "Dei delitti contro il patrimonio culturale"
