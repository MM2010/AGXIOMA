# Art. 339 codice penale - Circostanze aggravanti

## Summary
Art. 339 c.p. introduces aggravating circumstances for the crimes of violence or threat against a public official ([[art336-cp]]), resistance to a public official ([[art337-cp]]), and violence or threat against a political, administrative, or judicial body ([[art338-cp]]). The base penalties are increased when the conduct occurs in specific contexts—such as during public demonstrations, with weapons, by disguised persons, by groups, or using symbolic/intimidatory means. Additional, higher penalties apply when the violence involves large groups (more than five with arms, or more than ten without arms) or when it endangers people through thrown objects or fireworks. Finally, the aggravating factors also apply if the purpose is to prevent the construction or operation of essential public infrastructure (energy, transport, telecommunications, etc.).

## Key Details
- **General aggravating factors** (first comma): violence or threat committed:
  - during demonstrations in public or open-to-public places, or
  - with weapons, or
  - by a disguised person, or
  - by more than one person acting together (see [[art112-cp]] n.1), or
  - with an anonymous writing, or
  - in a symbolic manner, or
  - by exploiting the intimidating force of secret associations (real or supposed).
- **Higher penalties for large groups** (second comma):
  - If more than five persons are assembled and at least one uses a weapon → reclusion from 3 to 15 years (for first part of [[art336-cp]], [[art337-cp]], [[art338-cp]]); from 2 to 8 years (for second part of [[art336-cp]]).
  - If more than ten persons are assembled without any weapons → same increased penalties.
- **Use of thrown objects or fireworks** (third comma): the same higher penalties apply when violence or threat is carried out by throwing or using blunt objects or other offensive items (including pyrotechnics) in a way that creates danger to people (unless the act constitutes a more serious crime).
- **Infrastructure obstruction** (fourth comma): the general aggravating factors of the first comma also apply when the violence or threat aims to prevent the realization of infrastructure for energy, transport, telecommunications, or other public services.
- Reference articles for penalty increases: [[art64-cp]] (generic increase) and [[art585-cp]] (definition of weapons).
- The article explicitly links to the base offences: [[art336-cp]], [[art337-cp]], [[art338-cp]].
