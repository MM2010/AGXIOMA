# Art. 310 codice penale - Tempo di guerra

## Summary
Article 310 of the Italian Penal Code defines, for the purposes of criminal law, the term "time of war" as including also the period of imminent danger of war, provided that war actually follows. This provision is located in Book II, Title I (crimes against the personality of the state), Chapter V (general and common provisions to the preceding chapters). It extends the temporal scope of war-related criminal norms to cover preparatory periods when war is imminent, ensuring legal continuity.

## Key Details
- Article number: 310 (Codice Penale)
- Position: LIBRO SECONDO, Titolo I, Capo V (Disposizioni generali e comuni ai capi precedenti)
- Scope: Definition of "tempo di guerra" for all penal law purposes
- Inclusion: The period of imminent danger of war is considered wartime **only if** war actually follows (condicio iuris)
- Purpose: To allow application of war-related criminal provisions during the immediate pre-conflict phase
- Part of a set of general provisions applicable to crimes against the state's personality
