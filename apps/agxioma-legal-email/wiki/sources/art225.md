# Art. 225 codice penale - Minore imputabile

## Summary
Article 225 of the Italian Penal Code governs the application of personal security measures to minors aged 14 to 18 who have been declared imputable under [[art98-cp]]. After the minor has served the principal penalty, the judge may order their placement in a judicial reformatory or under supervised liberty, taking into account the same criteria listed in [[art224-cp]]. The measure is always mandatory if the minor is convicted of a crime while already subject to a security measure previously imposed for lack of imputability [[art227-cp]].

## Key Details
- Applies to minors between 14 and 18 years old who are recognized as imputable.
- The judge *may* order a security measure after the penalty has been executed (discretionary power).
- Two possible measures: commitment to a judicial reformatory, or assignment to supervised liberty (see [[art228-cp]] to [[art232-cp]]).
- The decision must consider the criteria of the first part of [[art224-cp]] (gravity of the act, personality, danger, etc.).
- The measure becomes *mandatory* when the minor is convicted for a crime committed *during the execution* of a previous security measure that was applied due to lack of imputability (i.e., the minor was then not imputable).
- This article interacts with the general regime of security measures for minors and the special rules on imputability.
