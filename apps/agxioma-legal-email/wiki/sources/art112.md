# Art. 112 codice penale - Circostanze aggravanti

## Summary
Art. 112 of the Italian Penal Code lists specific aggravating circumstances that increase the penalty for a crime committed by multiple participants. These apply when the number of participants is five or more, when the defendant promoted, organised or directed the cooperation, when the defendant used authority to induce subordinates, or when the defendant involved a minor under 18 or a person with mental infirmity. Further increases are provided for using non‑imputable or non‑punishable persons, and for parents exercising parental responsibility who induce such persons.

## Key Details
- The penalty is increased in the following cases: (1) five or more persons participated in the crime (unless otherwise provided by law); (2) the defendant promoted, organised or directed the cooperation; (3) the defendant, in exercising authority, direction or supervision, determined subordinates to commit the crime; (4) the defendant determined a minor under 18 or a person with mental infirmity to commit the crime, or availed themselves of or participated with such persons, for offences subject to arrest in flagrante delicto.
- For case (4), the increase is up to one‑half of the penalty.
- If the defendant used a non‑imputable or non‑punishable person (by reason of a personal condition or quality), the penalty is increased up to one‑half for offences subject to arrest in flagrante.
- If the defendant is the parent with parental responsibility who induced or used the person mentioned in cases (4) and the preceding paragraph, the increase is up to one‑half (first paragraph case 4) or up to two‑thirds (second paragraph).
- The aggravations under numbers 1, 2 and 3 also apply even if some participants are not imputable or are not punishable.
