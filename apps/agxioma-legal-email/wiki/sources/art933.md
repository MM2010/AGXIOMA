# Art. 933 Codice Civile: Rigetti del mare, piante sul lido, relitti aeronautici - Brocardi.it

## Summary

The article 933 of the Italian Civil Code (entitled "Rigetti del mare e piante sul lido. Relitti aeronautici") establishes that the rights over objects thrown into the sea or cast up by the sea, as well as over plants and grasses growing along the seashore, are governed by special laws (leggi speciali). The same principle applies to the recovery of aircraft and aircraft wreckage. This provision is part of the section on *occupazione* (occupation) and *invenzione* (treasure trove) within the modes of acquiring property, and it delegates the detailed regulation to sector-specific legislation (e.g., the Italian Navigation Code).

## Key Details

- **Article**: Art. 933, Codice Civile – Book III (Property), Title II, Capo III, Sezione I.
- **Location**: Part of the set of rules on acquisition of property through occupation and invention (articles 923‑933).
- **Subject**: Rights over (a) objects thrown into the sea or cast up by the sea, (b) plants/grasses on the seashore, (c) aircraft and aircraft wreckage.
- **Delegation**: All these matters are not regulated directly by the Civil Code but are deferred to **special laws** (leggi speciali).
- **Relevant special law**: Principally the *Codice della navigazione* (Italian Navigation Code, D.P.R. 15 febbraio 1952, n. 327, artt. 480‑496 and subsequent provisions).
- **Historical context**: The article maintains the traditional distinction between maritime finds (governed by maritime law) and land finds (governed by common law on invention/occupation).
