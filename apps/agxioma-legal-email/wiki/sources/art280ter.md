# Art. 280 ter codice penale - Atti di terrorismo nucleare

## Summary

This article of the Italian Penal Code (introduced by the Brocardi.it source) criminalizes nuclear terrorism acts. It punishes with reclusion not less than 15 years whoever, for terrorist purposes as defined by [[art270sexies]], procures radioactive material, creates or possesses a nuclear device. If the material or device is actually used, or if a nuclear plant is used or damaged in a way that releases radioactive matter (or creates a concrete risk thereof), the penalty is reclusion not less than 20 years. The same penalties apply when the conduct involves chemical or bacteriological agents.

## Key Details

- First paragraph: reclusion ≥ 15 years for procuring radioactive material, creating or possessing a nuclear device, with terrorist intent (as per [[art270sexies]]).
- Second paragraph: reclusion ≥ 20 years for using radioactive material or a nuclear device, or for using/damaging a nuclear plant causing release or concrete risk of release of radioactive matter.
- Third paragraph extends the penalties of both paragraphs to conduct involving chemical or bacteriological agents.
- The article is located in Book II, Title I, Chapter II of the Penal Code (crimes against the internal personality of the State).
