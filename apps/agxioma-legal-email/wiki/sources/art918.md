# Art. 918 codice civile - Consorzi volontari

## Summary
Art. 918 of the Italian Civil Code (Book III – Property, Title II – Ownership, Chapter II – Land Ownership, Section IX – Waters) allows owners of neighboring lands to form a voluntary consortium for the collective use of waters flowing from the same feeding basin or from contiguous basins. The adhesion of interested parties and the consortium regulations must be in writing (as required by [[art1350-cc]] n. 13). The regulations are adopted by a majority calculated according to the surface area of the lands served by the water.

## Key Details
- Enables the creation of a consortium among owners of **vicini fondi** (neighboring lands) to jointly use waters from the same or contiguous basins.
- The consortium is **voluntary**; no obligation to join arises from the article alone.
- **Adhesion** and the **consortium regulations** must be in writing under penalty of nullity ([[art1418-cc]]).
- Regulations are adopted by a **majority computed based on the extension** (surface area) of the lands that benefit from the water.
- The article is part of Section IX – “Delle acque” (Of Waters), which regulates water rights in the context of land property.
- The form requirement refers to [[art1350-cc]] n. 13 (acts requiring a public deed or authenticated private writing).
- The consortium is **private** in nature, distinct from public-law water consortia.
