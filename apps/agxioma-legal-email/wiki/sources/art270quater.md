# Art. 270 quater codice penale - Arruolamento con finalità di terrorismo anche internazionale

## Summary
Article 270 quater of the Italian Penal Code criminalizes the recruitment of one or more persons to commit acts of violence or sabotage of essential public services with terrorist intent, even if directed against a foreign state, an international institution, or an international organization. This provision applies outside the scope of [[art270bis-cp]] (associations with terrorist purposes) and establishes distinct penalties for the recruiter (seven to fifteen years' imprisonment) and the recruited person (five to eight years' imprisonment), unless the conduct falls under the training offense of [[art270quinquies-cp]]. The article is part of Title I (Crimes against the International Personality of the State) of Book II of the Penal Code.

## Key Details
- **Recruiter penalty**: imprisonment from 7 to 15 years.
- **Recruited person penalty**: imprisonment from 5 to 8 years (unless training under Art. 270 quinquies applies).
- Applies only *outside* the cases covered by [[art270bis-cp]] (terrorist associations).
- Requires recruitment for acts of **violence** or **sabotage of essential public services**.
- Terrorist purpose may be directed against Italy, a foreign state, an international institution, or an international organization.
- The offense is a specific crime distinct from membership in a terrorist group or individual training.
- Part of the anti-terrorism framework in the Italian Penal Code (Libro II, Titolo I, Capo I).
