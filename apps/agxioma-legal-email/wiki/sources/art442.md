# Art. 442 codice penale - Commercio di sostanze alimentari contraffatte o adulterate

## Summary
Article 442 of the Italian Penal Code punishes anyone who, without having directly participated in the offences of poisoning, corruption, adulteration or counterfeiting (provided for in the preceding three articles), detains for trade, markets, or distributes for consumption water, substances or things that have been altered by others in a manner dangerous to public health. The penalty is the same as that established for the principal offences under Articles 439, 440 and 441. This provision extends criminal liability to the downstream distribution chain, ensuring that intermediaries and traders are also held accountable for circulating dangerous products.

## Key Details
- Punishes any person who "detains for commerce, places on the market, or distributes for consumption" dangerous items already altered by others.
- Requires that the person has *not* participated in the original crimes of poisoning, corruption, adulteration, or counterfeiting.
- The item must be **dangerous to public health** (pericoloso alla salute pubblica).
- Applies to "acque, sostanze o cose" (waters, substances or things) – broadly covering food, drink, and similar consumables.
- Penalties are referred to from [[art439-cp]] (poisoning of water or food), [[art440-cp]] (adulteration or counterfeiting of food), and [[art441-cp]] (adulteration of other substances).
- Cross-reference also to [[art448-cp]], [[art452-cp]], and [[art516-cp]] (related offences).
- Part of Title VI ("Delitti contro l'incolumità pubblica"), Chapter II ("Dei delitti di comune pericolo mediante frode").
