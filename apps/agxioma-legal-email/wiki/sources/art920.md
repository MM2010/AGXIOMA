# Art. 920 codice civile - Norme applicabili

## Summary
Article 920 of the Italian Civil Code (Book III, Title II, Chapter II, Section IX – “Delle acque”) establishes that, save for what is provided in the preceding articles, the rules governing common ownership (*comunione*) apply to the voluntary water consortia referred to in those earlier provisions. This article acts as a residual rule, integrating the regime for water consortia with the general discipline of co‑ownership.

## Key Details
- **Full text**: “Salvo quanto è disposto dagli articoli precedenti, si applicano ai consorzi volontari ivi indicati le norme stabilite per la comunione.”
- **Position**: Codice Civile → Libro III (Della proprietà) → Titolo II (Della proprietà fondiaria) → Capo II → Sezione IX (Delle acque).
- **Function**: Provides a default legal framework for voluntary water consortia by referring to the rules on *comunione* (Articles 1100–1139 c.c.), unless specific derogations are contained in earlier articles of the same Section.
- **Scope**: Only concerns “consorzi volontari” (voluntary consortia) for water management, as distinct from mandatory consortia.
- **Relation to preceding articles**: Earlier articles in Sezione IX (e.g., 916–919 c.c.) contain special provisions for water rights, drainage, and consortia that take precedence over the common ownership rules.
- **Practical effect**: Absent specific regulation, members of a voluntary water consortium share rights and duties analogous to co‑owners, including management, expenses, and alienation.
