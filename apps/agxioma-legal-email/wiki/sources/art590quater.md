# Art. 590 quater codice penale - Computo delle circostanze

## Summary
Art. 590 quater establishes a mandatory priority rule in balancing multiple circumstances for selected serious crimes against life and personal safety (articles 589 bis, 589 ter, 590 bis, 590 ter). It provides that any attenuating circumstances—except those for age (article 98) or minor participation (article 114)—cannot be judged equivalent or prevalent over the specific aggravating circumstances. The reduction for attenuating circumstances is therefore applied only to the penalty determined after the aggravating circumstances have been computed, locking the aggregate penalty at a higher floor.

## Key Details
- Applies exclusively when aggravating circumstances under articles 589 bis (road homicide), 589 ter (maritime/air homicide), 590 bis (road personal injury), and 590 ter (maritime/air personal injury) occur.
- Excludes attenuating circumstances from articles 98 (minor age under 14) and 114 (minor participation) from the prohibition—these can still be equivalent or prevalent.
- For all other attenuating circumstances (e.g., mitigating causes in homicides or injuries), they cannot be ritenute equivalenti o prevalenti (considered equivalent or prevalent) relative to the listed aggravating ones.
- The practical effect: the court must apply the aggravating circumstance first, then subtract the attenuating reduction on that increased base, ensuring a higher minimum sentence.
- Reaffirms the principle of special laws (lex specialis) over general rules of balancing (art. 69 cp) for these specific crimes.
- The article was introduced by recent highway safety legislation (likely D.L. 23/2020 converted with modifications).
