# Art. 337 octies c.c. - Poteri del giudice e ascolto del minore

## Summary
Abrogated article of the Italian Civil Code (Libro I, Titolo IX, Capo II) that regulated the judge's powers of evidence-taking and mandatory hearing of the minor in proceedings concerning parental responsibility after separation, divorce, or annulment. It also provided for the possibility of postponing decisions to allow mediation between parents. The article was repealed by Legislative Decree 10 October 2022 n. 149 (civil process reform), as amended by Law 29 December 2022 n. 197.

## Key Details
- **Abrogation**: Effective from the entry into force of D. Lgs. 149/2022 (modified by L. 197/2022).
- **Scope**: Applied to proceedings under [[art337ter]] (provisions on custody and maintenance after separation).
- **Evidence**: The judge could order any necessary evidence, either *ex officio* or upon party request.
- **Child hearing**: Mandatory hearing of the minor who has turned 12, or younger if capable of discernment.
- **Exception to hearing**: Omitted if the hearing is contrary to the child’s best interests or manifestly superfluous (e.g., when the parents reach an agreement).
- **Mediation**: With the parties’ consent, the judge could postpone the adoption of measures to allow a mediation attempt, especially regarding the child’s moral and material interests.
- **Repealed provision**: The current rules on child hearing in family proceedings are now found in the reformed Code of Civil Procedure and related provisions (e.g., art. 473-bis.4 c.p.c. et seq.).
