# Art. 233 codice penale - Divieto di soggiorno in uno o più Comuni o in una o più Province

## Summary
This article of the Italian Penal Code establishes a personal security measure that prohibits the convicted person from residing in one or more municipalities or provinces designated by the judge. The measure applies to perpetrators of crimes against the personality of the state, against public order, crimes committed for political motives, or crimes occasioned by particular social or moral conditions existing in a specific place. The prohibition lasts at least one year; violation causes the minimum term to restart and may also lead to the imposition of supervised liberty (libertà vigilata).

## Key Details
- Applicable to: delitti contro la personalità dello Stato ([[art241-cp]]–[[art313-cp]]), delitti contro l’ordine pubblico ([[art414-cp]]–[[art421-cp]]), delitti commessi per motivi politici ([[art8-cp]]), or delitti occasionati da particolari condizioni sociali o morali locali.
- The judge designates the specific Comuni or Province where residence is forbidden.
- Minimum duration: one year.
- Violation of the prohibition: the minimum term begins to run anew from the date of violation; additionally, the court may order libertà vigilata (supervised liberty).
- Classified as a personal security measure (misura di sicurezza personale) under Title VIII of Book I of the Penal Code.
