# Art. 649 codice penale - Non punibilità e querela per fatti commessi a danno di congiunti

## Summary
Art. 649 c.p. introduces a special regime of non-punibility and procedural bar (requirement of querela) for property crimes committed against certain close family members. It excludes punishment for theft, fraud, misappropriation, and similar offenses when the victim is the non-legally‑separated spouse, the cohabiting union partner (unione civile), an ancestor/descendant, an in‑law in direct line, an adoptive parent/adoptee, or a cohabiting sibling. For other listed relatives (e.g., legally separated spouse, non‑cohabiting sibling, uncle/nephew, affine in secondo grado), the crime is actionable only upon complaint (querela). The immunity does not apply to violent property crimes (robbery, extortion, kidnapping for ransom).

## Key Details
- **Non‑punibility** (no crime) applies for acts against: spouse not legally separated ([[art150-151-cc]]), part of an unione civile, ascendant/descendant, affine in line retta, adopter/adoptee ([[art291-cc]]), cohabiting brother/sister.
- **Querela‑only** (punished only upon complaint) for acts against: legally separated spouse, union partner after declaration of dissolution but before final dissolution, non‑cohabiting brother/sister, cohabiting uncle/nephew, cohabiting affine in secondo grado.
- **Excepted crimes** (full punishability): [[art628-cp]] (robbery), [[art629-cp]] (extortion), [[art630-cp]] (kidnapping for ransom), and any other property crime committed with violence against persons.
- The article is part of *Capo III – Disposizioni comuni ai capi precedenti* of *Titolo XIII – Dei delitti contro il patrimonio*.
- The rationale is to preserve family harmony and avoid penal intervention in intra‑family property disputes, except when violence is used.
- The query refers to relations as defined in the Civil Code (affinity, adoption, separation).
