# Art. 561 codice penale - Casi di non punibilità. Circostanza attenuante

## Summary
Article 561 of the Italian Penal Code provided for cases of non‑punishability and a mitigating circumstance for the crimes of adultery ([[art559]] – art. 559 cp) and concubinage ([[art560]] – art. 560 cp). It excluded punishment for a wife if her husband induced or profited from her prostitution, and for a spouse legally separated through the fault of the other or unjustly abandoned; it also reduced the penalty if the separation occurred by mutual consent or the acting spouse’s own fault. The entire article was declared constitutionally illegitimate by the Constitutional Court with judgment no. 147 of 3 December 1969, rendering it void and historically obsolete.

## Key Details
- Article belonged to Title XI (crimes against the family), Capo I (crimes against marriage).
- Applied to the now‑repealed crimes of adultery (art. 559) and concubinage (art. 560).
- Contained two distinct non‑punishability cases and one mitigating circumstance.
- The non‑punishability for the wife was conditional on the husband’s inducement, incitement, or advantage from her prostitution.
- The non‑punishability for either spouse applied when separated through the other’s fault or unjust abandonment.
- The mitigating circumstance applied when separation was due to the acting spouse’s own fault or by mutual consent.
- Declared constitutionally illegitimate by Corte Costituzionale sent. 3 dicembre 1969, n. 147, on grounds of violating the equality principle (art. 3 Cost.).
- The declaration of illegitimacy eliminated the entire article from the legal system retroactively.
- No longer in force; relevant only for historical and jurisprudential study.
