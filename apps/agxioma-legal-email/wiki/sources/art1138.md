# Art. 1138 c.c. - Regolamento di condominio

## Summary
This article of the Italian Civil Code mandates the creation of a condominium regulation (regolamento di condominio) in buildings with more than ten unit owners. It prescribes the minimum content of the regulation (use of common property, cost allocation, protection of building decor, administration rules), the approval procedure (by assembly majority as per [[art1136]]), and the possibility of challenge (per [[art1107]]). The article also sets limits: the regulation cannot infringe individual property rights from purchase deeds or derogate from listed fundamental provisions, and it cannot prohibit the keeping of domestic animals.

## Key Details
- Mandatory regulation when the number of condomini exceeds 10 (obbligatorio)
- Must contain rules on: use of common things ([[art1117]]), expense sharing proportional to rights and duties, protection of building decor, and administration ([[art68]], [[art72]])
- Any condomino can initiate formation or revision
- Approved by assembly with the majority of [[art1136]] comma 2
- Attached to the register of [[art1130]] n. 7
- Can be impugned under [[art1107]]
- Cannot diminish rights derived from purchase deeds or conventions
- Cannot derogate from articles: 1118 co. 2, 1119, 1120, 1129, 1131, 1132, 1136, 1137 ([72], [155])
- Explicit prohibition on banning domestic animals (animali domestici)
