# Art. 1044 Codice Civile – Bonifica

## Summary
Article 1044 of the Italian Civil Code grants a landowner who wishes to drain or reclaim his land (e.g., by ditches, fillings, or other means) the right to channel runoff waters through neighbouring properties to a watercourse or outlet, provided he pays an indemnity and minimises damage. When the drainage conflicts with the interests of those who use the waters from the swampy land, the judicial authority balances the competing interests, prioritising the general production needs, and may award a fair indemnity to the opposing parties.

## Key Details
- Applies to drainage/reclamation works (prosciugamento o bonifica) using sewers, ditches, fills, or similar methods.
- The landowner must pay an indemnity and cause the least possible harm to the servient tenement.
- The right is subject to laws on land reclamation (bonifica) and forest constraints (vincolo forestale).
- If drainage conflicts with users of the waters from the marshy land, the court decides which interest prevails, taking into account general production needs.
- When the conflict cannot be resolved by proportionate works, the court may order drainage and assign a fair indemnity to the opponents.
- This is a **coattive servitude** (servitù coattiva) – a forced right over another’s property for public or private utility.
