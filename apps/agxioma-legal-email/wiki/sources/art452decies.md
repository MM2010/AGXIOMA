# Art. 452 decies codice penale - Ravvedimento operoso

## Summary
Article 452 decies of the Italian Penal Code (introduced by Law 68/2015 on environmental crimes) provides significant sentence reductions for offenders who actively cooperate to prevent further harm, carry out remediation, or assist authorities in investigating environmental crimes. The norm also allows the judge to suspend proceedings for up to three years to permit such remedial actions, thereby suspending the statute of limitations. This "active repentance" mechanism aims to encourage prompt restoration of environmental damage and efficient law enforcement.

## Key Details
- The penalties for environmental crimes under Title VI-bis, for the aggravated mafia-type association under [[art416-cp]] in conjunction with [[art452octies-cp]], and for the crime of illegal waste trafficking under art. 260 D.Lgs. 152/2006, are reduced by **one-half to two-thirds** if the offender:
  - actively works to prevent further criminal consequences; or
  - before the opening of first-instance trial, concretely carries out securing, remediation, and, where possible, restoration of the site.
- The penalties are reduced by **one-third to one-half** if the offender concretely assists police or judicial authorities in reconstructing the facts, identifying perpetrators, or seizing resources relevant to the crimes.
- On the defendant's request, the judge may suspend the proceedings for a period not exceeding **two years**, extendable by a maximum of **one additional year**, to allow ongoing remediation activities; the statute of limitations is suspended during such suspension.
- The provision applies only to the specific environmental crimes listed (Title VI-bis, aggravated 416, art. 260 TUA) and not to all Penal Code offenses.
- The remedial actions must be "concrete" and effectively carried out, not merely promised.
