# Art. 787 codice civile - Errore sul motivo della donazione

## Summary
Article 787 of the Italian Civil Code governs the impugnment of a donation on grounds of error concerning the motive that induced the donor to make the liberalità. The error – whether of fact or of law – must result from the deed and be the sole determining cause of the donation. This remedy is tightly circumscribed to protect the stability of donations while acknowledging the subjective importance of the donor’s intention.

## Key Details
- The donation can be challenged only if the erroneous motive appears from the act itself and was the exclusive reason for the liberalità (not merely one of several motives).
- The error may be either factual or legal; both are treated equally.
- The provision explicitly cross-references Article 624, comma 2 (applicable to testaments) and Articles 1428 ff. (general rules on error in contracts), as well as Article 794 (modal donations).
- Unlike ordinary contracts, where error must be essential and recognizable, here the error need only be the exclusive, documented motive.
- The burden of proof lies with the donor (or their heirs) seeking annulment.
- The action is subject to the ordinary prescriptive period (10 years from the date of the donation, unless otherwise specified).
