# Art. 2049 codice civile - Responsabilità dei padroni e dei committenti - Brocardi.it

## Summary
[[art2049]] introduces a strict liability regime for employers (padroni) and principals (committenti) for damages caused by the tortious acts of their domestic workers or employees (domestici e commessi), provided the acts occur while performing the assigned duties. This article, located in Book IV, Title IX of the Italian Civil Code (fatti illeciti), shifts the burden of proof from the injured party to the employer, who cannot avoid liability by claiming lack of fault in supervision or selection. The rule applies both to employment relationships and broader situations where one party entrusts tasks to another.

## Key Details
- **Article reference**: Art. 2049 c.c. – "Responsabilità dei padroni e dei committenti"
- **Nature**: Objective liability (responsabilità oggettiva) – no need to prove employer’s fault or culpa in vigilando/in eligendo.
- **Scope**: Damages caused by servants, domestic staff, or employees (commessi) acting within the scope of their assigned duties (nell'esercizio delle incombenze).
- **Conditions**: (1) Existence of a relationship of preposizione (superior-subordinate); (2) The tortious act must be committed in the exercise of the entrusted functions; (3) Damage must result directly from that act.
- **Damages**: The liability extends to all damages recoverable under [[art2056]] and subsequent articles ([[art2056-cc|2056 c.c.]] ff.).
- **Relationship with other provisions**: The article cross-references [[art1900]] (contractual liability for work performed by others) and is part of the broader framework on tort liability ([[art2043]] ff.).
- **Case law interpretation**: Courts interpret "incombenze" broadly to include acts that are even remotely connected to the assigned tasks, unless the employee acts for purely personal reasons.
