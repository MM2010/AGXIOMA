# Art. 744 codice civile - Perimento della cosa donata

## Summary
Art. 744 of the Italian Civil Code provides an exemption from the obligation of collation (collazione) in inheritance proceedings. It states that a donated item that has perished without fault attributable to the donee is not subject to collation. This means the donee is not required to return the item's value to the estate for the purpose of equalizing shares among heirs, aligning with the general principle of impossibility of performance under Art. 1256 c.c.

## Key Details
- The exemption applies only when the destruction of the donated thing is not attributable to the donee (i.e., force majeure or accident).
- The reference to [[art1256-codice-civile]] links the concept of non-imputable impossibility.
- Collation (collazione) is the duty to bring donated property back into the estate for equal distribution among forced heirs.
- This article is part of the division of estates (Titolo IV, Capo II) under the Book of Successions (Libro Secondo).
- The rule protects the donee from being unfairly burdened when the donated asset no longer exists through no fault of their own.
