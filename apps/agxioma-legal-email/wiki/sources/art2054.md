# Art. 2054 codice civile - Circolazione di veicoli

## Summary
Art. 2054 of the Italian Civil Code governs civil liability arising from the circulation of vehicles not guided by rails. It establishes a presumption of fault against the driver, who is liable unless they prove they did everything possible to avoid the damage. In collisions, each driver is presumed to have contributed equally. The vehicle owner (or usufructuary/purchaser under retention of title) is jointly and severally liable unless the vehicle was driven against their will. Liability also extends to defects in construction or maintenance.

## Key Details
- The driver bears the burden of proof to exonerate themselves by showing all possible measures were taken to avoid harm.
- In a collision between two vehicles, both drivers are presumed equally responsible unless one proves otherwise.
- The owner, usufructuary, or conditional buyer (art. 1523) is jointly liable unless they prove the vehicle was used without their consent.
- All responsible parties are also liable for damages caused by construction defects or lack of maintenance.
- The article applies to any vehicle without rails (e.g., cars, motorcycles, bicycles) in motion on public or private roads.
