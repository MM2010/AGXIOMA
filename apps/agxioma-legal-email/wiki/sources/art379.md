# Art. 379 codice penale - Favoreggiamento reale

## Summary
The article punishes **favoreggiamento reale** (real aiding), i.e., helping a person to secure the product, profit, or price of a crime, outside the cases of complicity ([[art110-cp]]) and the specific offences of receiving stolen goods ([[art648-cp]]), money laundering ([[art648bis-cp]]), and self‑laundering ([[art648ter-cp]]). The penalty is imprisonment up to five years if the underlying crime is a delitto, or a fine from €51 to €1,032 if it is a contravvenzione. It also makes applicable the same aggravating and mitigating circumstances provided for in [[art378-cp]] (favoreggiamento personale).

## Key Details
- **Offence**: Aiding someone to secure the product, profit, or price of a crime (real favouring).
- **Exclusions**: Does not apply when the aider is a co‑perpetrator of the original crime ([[art110-cp]]) or when the conduct falls under [[art648-cp]] (ricettazione), [[art648bis-cp]] (riciclaggio), or [[art648ter-cp]] (impiego di denaro illecito).
- **Penalty for delitto**: reclusione up to 5 years.
- **Penalty for contravvenzione**: multa da €51 a €1.032.
- **Circumstances**: The provisions of the first and last paragraphs of the preceding article ([[art378-cp]]) apply, therefore the penalty may be increased if the underlying crime is particularly serious or decreased in certain situations.
- **Systematic position**: Libro II, Titolo III, Capo I (“Dei delitti contro l’attività giudiziaria”).
- **Complementarity**: Works alongside [[art378-cp]] (favoreggiamento personale) which covers aiding a person to evade investigations.
