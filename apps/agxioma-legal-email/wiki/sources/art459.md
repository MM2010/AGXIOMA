# Art. 459 codice penale - Falsificazione dei valori di bollo, introduzione nello Stato, acquisto, detenzione o messa in circolazione di valori di bollo falsificati - Brocardi.it

## Summary
Article 459 of the Italian Penal Code extends the criminal provisions of articles 453, 455, and 457 (concerning counterfeiting and circulation of false money, public credit instruments, and foreign currency) to the counterfeiting or alteration of revenue stamps (*valori di bollo*), as well as to their introduction into the State, purchase, possession, or circulation. However, the penalties are reduced by one third. The article also provides a legal definition of "valori di bollo," which includes stamped paper, revenue stamps, postage stamps, and other equivalent instruments designated by special laws.

## Key Details
- Applies the same criminal framework as [[art453]], [[art455]], and [[art457]] to revenue stamps.
- Criminalized conduct: counterfeiting, alteration, introduction into Italian territory, purchase, possession, and circulation of counterfeit or altered revenue stamps.
- Penalties are reduced by one third compared to those for currency counterfeiting.
- Defines "valori di bollo" as: stamped paper, revenue stamps, postage stamps, and any other items equated to them by special laws.
- The article is part of Title VII (Crimes against public faith), Chapter I (Falsehood in coins, public credit instruments, and revenue stamps).
