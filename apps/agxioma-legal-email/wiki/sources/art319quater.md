# Art. 319 quater codice penale - Induzione indebita a dare o promettere utilità

## Summary
Art. 319 quater of the Italian Penal Code (introduced by Law 190/2012) punishes the so-called "induzione indebita" – a public official or person in charge of a public service who, abusing their quality or powers, induces someone to give or promise money or other undue benefit to themselves or a third party. The crime bridges the areas of concussione (Art. 317) and corruzione (Art. 319), with the induced party also receiving a (lower) penalty. The offense is particularly relevant in cases of induced bribery and in the context of EU financial interests.

## Key Details
- **Act**: The public official or incaricato di pubblico servizio, by abusing their quality or powers, induces another to give or promise unduly money or other utility.
- **Penalty (inducer)**: Reclusione from 6 years to 10 years and 6 months.
- **Penalty (induced giver/promiser)**: Up to 3 years of reclusione; up to 4 years if the offense offends EU financial interests and the damage or profit exceeds €100,000.
- **Element**: Abuse of the official’s quality or powers is required – mere solicitation without such abuse is insufficient.
- **Location**: Book II, Title II (Crimes against the public administration), Chapter I (Crimes of public officials).
- **Distinguished from Art. 317 (concussione)**: In concussione the official *forces* the victim; here the victim is *induced* and also punished (as a co-participant, but with a mitigated penalty).
- **Relation to corruzione**: Unlike classic corruption (Art. 319), the initiative comes from the official who abuses his position, not a bilateral agreement.
- **Scope**: The undue benefit may be given to the official or to a third party.
