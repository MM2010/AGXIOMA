# Art. 1053 codice civile - Indennità

## Summary
Article 1053 of the Italian Civil Code, located in the section on compulsory easements (servitù coattive), establishes the indemnity due to the owner of the servient estate when a compulsory right of passage is granted under the conditions set by the two preceding articles (1051 and 1052). The indemnity must be proportional to the damage caused by the passage. If the passage requires stable works or leaves a part of the servient estate uncultivated, the applicant must pay the value of that area (calculated according to the first paragraph of Art. 1038) before starting the works or the passage itself.

## Key Details
- Indemnity is proportional to the damage caused by the passage (danno cagionato).
- A special rule applies when the passage necessitates stable works (opere stabili) or leaving a zone of the servient estate uncultivated.
- In such cases, the owner requesting the passage must pay the value of that affected zone *before* commencing the works or the passage.
- The value is determined in accordance with the first paragraph of Article 1038.
- Article 1053 complements the provisions of Articles 1051 and 1052, which govern the conditions for granting a compulsory passage.
