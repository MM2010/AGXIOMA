# Art. 71 codice penale - Condanna per più reati con unica sentenza o decreto

## Summary
Art. 71 of the Italian Penal Code serves as the introductory provision for the discipline of concurrent crimes (concorso di reati). It mandates that when a single judgment (sentenza) or decree (decreto) must pronounce a conviction for multiple offenses against the same person, the rules set out in the following articles (Art. 72–??) shall be applied. This article defines the scope of the subsequent normative framework governing the accumulation of penalties and the treatment of multiple convictions.

## Key Details
- Located in Book I, Title III, Chapter III of the Penal Code.
- Applies when a single **sentenza** (judgment) or **decreto penale** (criminal decree) imposes conviction for more than one crime against the same individual.
- Does not define the penalties themselves, but triggers the application of the following provisions (e.g., material cumulation, legal cumulation – Art. 72–??).
- The article explicitly uses the expression “più reati” (multiple crimes), thus encompassing both **material concourse** (concorso materiale) and **formal concourse** (concorso formale), as further detailed in subsequent norms.
- It is a procedural-penal rule, linking the single act of judgment to the substantive rules on penalty accumulation.
- The phrase “contro la stessa persona” clarifies that the provision applies only when the same defendant is convicted of several crimes.
