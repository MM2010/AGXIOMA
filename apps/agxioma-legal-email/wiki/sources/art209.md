# Art. 209 codice penale - Persona giudicata per più fatti

## Summary
Art. 209 c.p. regulates the cumulative application of personal security measures (misure di sicurezza personali) when a person has committed multiple criminal acts. It establishes the principle of unification for measures of the same kind, and a discretionary global assessment by the judge for measures of different kinds, always ensuring that detentive measures based on presumed danger (under [[art204-cp]]) are mandatorily applied. This article applies both at sentencing and during the execution phase.

## Key Details
- If multiple acts give rise to **security measures of the same species**, only **one measure** is ordered (cumulo giuridico).
- If measures are **of different species**, the judge must carry out a **global assessment of the person’s dangerousness** and may apply one or more of the measures provided by law ([art. 219 c.p.]).
- **Detentive security measures** (e.g., assignment to a colony, psychiatric hospital) are **always** applied when the subject is subject to them by law on the basis of **presumed danger** (pericolo presunto, [[art204-cp]]).
- The same rules apply when the security measures are **already being executed** or have **not yet begun**.
- The provision is part of the **general rules on personal security measures** (Titolo VIII, Capo I, Sezione I c.p.).
