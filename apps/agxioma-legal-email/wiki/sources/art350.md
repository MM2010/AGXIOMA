# Art. 350 codice penale - Agevolazione colposa

## Summary
Art. 350 of the Italian Penal Code punishes any private individual who, through negligence (colpa), facilitates or makes possible the violation of seals placed on property. Unlike the intentional violation of seals (Art. 349 cp), this article addresses only the negligent conduct of the custodian and imposes an administrative pecuniary sanction, not a criminal penalty. The provision is part of the offenses against the public administration (Book II, Title II, Chapter II).

## Key Details
- **Normative source:** Italian Penal Code, Book II, Title II, Chapter II.
- **Offense:** Negligent facilitation or enabling of the violation of seals.
- **Subject:** Only the person who has custody of the sealed item (the custodian).
- **Mental element:** Negligence (colpa) as defined in [[art43-cp]].
- **Sanction:** Administrative pecuniary fine from €154 to €929 (as of the last amendment).
- **Relation to other articles:** This is a minor (colposa) counterpart to the intentional violation of seals (Art. 349 cp) and to the aggravated form in Art. 351 cp.
- **Purpose:** To protect the integrity of public seals and the trust placed in custodians.
