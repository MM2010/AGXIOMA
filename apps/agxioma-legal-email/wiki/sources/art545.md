# Art. 545 codice penale - Aborto di donna non consenziente

## Summary
Article 545 of the Italian Penal Code originally criminalised inducing an abortion without the woman's consent, punishable by seven to twelve years' imprisonment. This provision, part of Title X (crimes against the integrity and health of the lineage), was entirely abrogated by Art. 22 of Law 22 May 1978, n. 194, which legalised voluntary termination of pregnancy under regulated conditions. The article is now of historical interest only.

## Key Details
- Original text: *"Chiunque cagiona l'aborto di una donna, senza il consenso di lei, è punito con la reclusione da sette a dodici anni."*
- Penalty: imprisonment from 7 to 12 years.
- Article belonged to Title X of Book II of the Penal Code (now repealed).
- Abrogated by Art. 22, L. 22 maggio 1978, n. 194 (the Italian abortion law).
- No longer in force; current regulation of abortion is governed entirely by Law 194/1978.
- The provision addressed only the case of non-consensual abortion; consensual abortion was covered by previous articles (e.g., Art. 544, also abrogated).
