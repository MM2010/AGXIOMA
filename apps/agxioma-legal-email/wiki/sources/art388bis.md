# Art. 388 bis codice penale - Violazione colposa dei doveri inerenti alla custodia di cose sottoposte a pignoramento ovvero a sequestro giudiziario o conservativo

## Summary
This article of the Italian Penal Code punishes the negligent breach of duties related to the custody of items subject to seizure (pignoramento) or judicial/conservative sequestration (sequestro giudiziario o conservativo). It establishes that any custodian who, through negligence, causes the destruction or dispersion of such items, or facilitates their suppression or subtraction, is liable to imprisonment up to six months or a fine up to €309, upon complaint (querela) of the offended party. The provision is located within the chapter on crimes against the authority of judicial decisions, complementing the intentional version under art. 388 cp.

## Key Details
- Article number: 388 bis (Codice Penale, Book II, Title III, Chapter II)
- Conduct: *colposa* (negligent) violation of custodial duties — destruction, dispersion, facilitation of suppression or subtraction
- Offense subject: items under *pignoramento* (attachment) or *sequestro giudiziario/conservativo* (judicial/conservatory sequestration)
- Punishment: reclusione fino a 6 mesi **o** multa fino a €309 (alternative penalty)
- Procedural requirement: proceedability only upon *querela della persona offesa*
- Location in code: [[art388-cp]] (intentional violation) is the immediate predecessor; followed by [[art388ter-cp]] (if exists, otherwise indicate succession)
- Distinguish from intentional version: art. 388 cp punishes the same acts committed *dolosamente*; art. 388 bis covers the corresponding negligent form
- Scope: applies to any custodian legally entrusted with such items (e.g., judicial custodian, debtor, sequestered party)
