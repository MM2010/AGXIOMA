# Art. 473 codice penale - Contraffazione, alterazione o uso di marchi o segni distintivi ovvero di brevetti, modelli e disegni

## Summary
This article of the Italian Penal Code criminalises the counterfeiting, alteration, or use of counterfeit trademarks, distinctive signs, patents, designs, and industrial models, both national and foreign. It establishes different penalties depending on whether the infringement concerns trademarks (imprisonment from six months to three years and a fine from €2,500 to €25,000) or industrial property rights (imprisonment from one to four years and a fine from €3,500 to €35,000). The offence is punishable only if domestic, EU, or international rules on intellectual or industrial property protection have been observed.

## Key Details
- Punishes anyone who, being able to know of the existence of the industrial property title, counterfeits or alters trademarks or distinctive signs of industrial products, or uses such counterfeit or altered marks.
- Heavier penalties apply to counterfeiting or altering patents, industrial designs, or models, as well as using them without having participated in the counterfeiting.
- Penalties for trademarks: imprisonment from 6 months to 3 years and fine from €2,500 to €25,000.
- Penalties for patents, designs, models: imprisonment from 1 to 4 years and fine from €3,500 to €35,000.
- Both national and foreign marks/patents/designs/models are covered.
- The offence requires that the perpetrator had the possibility to know of the existence of the industrial property title.
- Applicability is conditional on compliance with domestic laws, EU regulations, and international conventions on intellectual or industrial property protection.
- The article is part of Chapter II ("Falsity in seals, instruments or marks of authentication, certification or recognition") of Title VII ("Crimes against public faith").
