# Art. 671 cod. pen. - Impiego di minori nell'accattonaggio

## Summary
Article 671 of the Italian Penal Code, abrogated by Law no. 94 of 15 July 2009, criminalised the use of minors under the age of fourteen or persons not criminally imputable for begging, provided the minor was under the subject's authority, custody, or supervision. The offence carried imprisonment from three months to one year; if committed by a parent or guardian, the sentence also entailed suspension of parental authority or guardianship. The provision was part of the third book of the Code (contraventions) and was repealed as part of a broader reform of public order offences.

## Key Details
- Located in Book III, Title I, Chapter I, Section I of the Italian Penal Code (contraventions concerning public order and public tranquility).
- Prohibition: using a minor under 14 or a non‑imputable person for begging, or allowing such use by others.
- The minor had to be subject to the offender's authority, custody, or vigilance.
- Punishment: arrest from 3 months to 1 year.
- Aggravated effect: if the offender was the parent or guardian, conviction automatically suspended parental authority or guardianship.
- Abrogated by art. 3, comma 19, letter d) of Law 94/2009 (so‑called "Pacchetto sicurezza").
- No transitional provision was made; the conduct is no longer a stand‑alone contravention under this article.
