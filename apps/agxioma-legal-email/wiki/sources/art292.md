# Art. 292 codice penale - Vilipendio o danneggiamento alla bandiera o ad altro emblema dello Stato

## Summary
Art. 292 of the Italian Penal Code punishes acts of vilipendio (verbal insults) and danneggiamento (physical destruction, deterioration, or soiling) directed at the national flag or other State emblems. It belongs to Title I "Delitti contro la personalità dello Stato", Chapter II "Delitti contro la personalità interna dello Stato". The provision distinguishes two levels of severity: simple vilipendio carries a fine of €1,000–€5,000 (aggravated to €5,000–€10,000 if committed during a public celebration or official ceremony), while public and intentional damaging of the flag or emblem is punishable by imprisonment up to two years.

## Key Details
- Two distinct offences: (1) vilipendio (insulting expressions) and (2) danneggiamento (physical destruction, dispersion, deterioration, rendering unusable, or soiling).
- Vilipendio penalty: fine from €1,000 to €5,000; increased to €5,000–€10,000 if committed during a public celebration or official ceremony.
- Danneggiamento penalty: reclusione (imprisonment) up to two years, requiring public and intentional conduct.
- "Bandiera nazionale" is defined as the official State flag or any flag bearing the national colours (cf. [[Costituzione Italiana]] art. 12).
- "Altro emblema dello Stato" covers coats of arms, official seals, and similar symbols.
- The article is part of the chapter on offences against the internal personality of the State.
- No distinction between Italian and foreign flags is made in this article (foreign flags are covered by other provisions, e.g., art. 299 cp).
- The crime is of a purely intentional nature (dolo generico).
