# Art. 1181 codice civile – Adempimento parziale

## Summary
Art. 1181 of the Italian Civil Code governs the creditor’s right to refuse partial performance even when the obligation is divisible, unless the law or custom provides otherwise. This provision reinforces the principle that the creditor is entitled to full and exact performance, protecting the creditor’s interest in receiving the entire prestation as agreed.

## Key Details
- The creditor may reject partial performance even if the performance is divisible by nature.
- Exceptions exist where the law or trade usage allows the debtor to perform in installments or part deliveries.
- Relevant exceptions include: installment sales ([[art1484-cc]]), performance of divisible works ([[art1672-cc]]), general provisions on obligations under the Civil Code, and specific laws such as the check law (art. 45 R.D. 1736/1933) and insurance code (art. 37 R.D. 295/1942).
- The article reflects the principle of integral performance ([[art1181-cc]]) and is linked to the concept of partial performance in the context of divisible obligations.
- It is located in Book IV (Obbligazioni), Title I, Chapter II, Section I of the Italian Civil Code.
