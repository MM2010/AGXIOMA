# Art. 452 duodecies codice penale - Ripristino dello stato dei luoghi

## Summary
This article (inserted by Legislative Decree 121/2011) governs the restoration of the environment following conviction for any of the environmental crimes under Title VI‑bis of the Italian Penal Code. Upon a conviction or a plea agreement (applicazione della pena su richiesta delle parti ex art. 444 c.p.p.), the judge must order the recovery and, where technically possible, the restoration of the site to its original state. The execution is imposed on the convicted person and on any entities liable under art. 197 c.p. The restoration is carried out in accordance with the environmental remediation provisions of Legislative Decree 152/2006 (Codice dell'Ambiente).

## Key Details
- Applies to definitive convictions or plea‑bargaining sentences for any crime under Title VI‑bis (environmental delicts).
- The judge **must** order recovery and, if technically feasible, full restoration of the place.
- Costs and execution are borne by the convicted person and by the persons/entities referred to in [[art197-cp]] (civil liability for fines and reparation).
- The restoration procedure follows the rules of Part VI, Title II of D.Lgs. 152/2006 (remediation of contaminated sites and environmental damage).
- The provision aims to ensure concrete environmental reparation as a necessary consequence of the crime, not merely a penalty.
- Technical impossibility of full restoration does not relieve the obligation to recover (clean‑up, removal of hazards, etc.).
