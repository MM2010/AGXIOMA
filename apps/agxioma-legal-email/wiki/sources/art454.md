# Art. 454 codice penale - Alterazione di monete

## Summary
This article of the Italian Penal Code punishes anyone who alters coins of the kind specified in the preceding article (Art. 453), reducing their value in any way, or who commits any of the acts indicated in numbers 3 and 4 of that article with respect to such altered coins. The penalty is imprisonment from one to five years and a fine ranging from €103 to €516. It is part of Title VII (Crimes against public faith), Chapter I (Falsehood in coins, public credit instruments, and stamped values).

## Key Details
- The article refers to coins of the "quality" indicated in [[art453]] (falsification of coins).
- "Alteration" means any reduction of the coin's value (e.g., clipping, filing, chemical reduction).
- It also criminalizes the same acts listed in Art. 453, numbers 3 and 4: spending, circulating, or introducing such altered coins into the territory; or selling or otherwise placing them into circulation.
- The punishment is reclusion (imprisonment) from 1 to 5 years, plus a fine from €103 to €516.
- The article protects the integrity of the monetary system and public trust in legal tender.
