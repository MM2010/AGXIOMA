# Art. 75 codice penale - Concorso di reati che importano pene pecuniarie di specie diversa

## Summary
Article 75 of the Italian Penal Code regulates the concurrent imposition of different types of pecuniary penalties when a single person commits multiple crimes. It establishes that penalties of different species (multa and ammenda) must be applied separately and in full, without any merging or limitation. In case of partial payment, the amount already paid is deducted from the multa for the purpose of conversion into other forms of punishment.

## Key Details
- Applies when multiple crimes each require pecuniary penalties of different species (multa for delitti, ammenda for contravvenzioni).
- The penalties are applied cumulatively — each is enforced separately and for its entire amount.
- If the pecuniary penalty is not fully paid, the sum actually paid is subtracted from the multa before conversion (e.g., into imprisonment).
- The rule is part of the broader framework on concorso di reati (concurrence of offenses) in the Italian Penal Code.
- This article complements Art. 74, which deals with pecuniary penalties of the same species (where a single maximum applies).
- Conversion of unpaid pecuniary penalties is governed by Art. 102 and related provisions.
