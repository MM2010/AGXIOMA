# Art. 919 codice civile - Scioglimento del consorzio

## Summary
This article of the Italian Civil Code regulates the dissolution of a consortium (consorzio) concerning water rights within the framework of land property. It establishes that dissolution requires a supermajority vote of more than three‑fourths of the members, or – if division can be carried out without serious damage – it may be requested by a single interested party.

## Key Details
- The article is part of **Book III (Property)**, Title II (Land Property), Chapter II, Section IX (Waters).
- Dissolution of the consortium is not permitted unless either:
  - A majority exceeding three‑fourths (75%+) of the members votes in favour, or
  - An interested party demands it, provided that the division of the common property can be effected without grave damage.
- The provision balances collective decision‑making with individual rights, allowing breakup only when the collective interest is overwhelming or when no serious harm results.
- “Consorzio” in this context refers to a mandatory or voluntary association of landowners for the management of water resources (e.g., irrigation, drainage).
