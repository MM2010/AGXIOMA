# Art. 609 sexies codice penale - Ignoranza dell'età della persona offesa

## Summary
This article (609 sexies of the Italian Penal Code) provides a strict liability rule regarding the age of the victim for several sexual offenses involving minors under eighteen. It states that the perpetrator cannot invoke ignorance of the victim’s age as a defense, unless the ignorance was unavoidable (i.e., inevitable despite all due diligence). The rule applies to the crimes set forth in Articles 609 bis (sexual violence), 609 ter (aggravated sexual violence), 609 quater (sexual acts with a minor), 609 octies (group sexual violence), 609 undecies (other sexual offenses against minors), and 609 quinquies (corruption of a minor). This provision reinforces the protection of minors by shifting the burden to the defendant to prove that ignorance of age was not culpable.

## Key Details
- Applies only when the victim is under eighteen years of age.
- Covers: [[art609-bis]], [[art609-ter]], [[art609-quater]], [[art609-octies]], [[art609-undecies]], and [[art609-quinquies]].
- The defendant cannot use ignorance of the victim’s age as an excuse unless that ignorance was **inevitable** (i.e., objectively impossible to avoid even with reasonable diligence).
- The exception of “inevitable ignorance” is extremely narrow and rarely successful in practice; the burden of proof rests on the accused.
- No distinction is made between the minor’s age (e.g., under 14, under 16, under 18) – the rule uniformly applies to any offense committed against a person under eighteen.
- This article is part of Section II (Dei delitti contro la libertà personale) within Chapter III (Dei delitti contro la libertà individuale) of Title XII of Book II of the Penal Code.
