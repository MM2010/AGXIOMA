# Art. 798 codice civile - Responsabilità per vizi della cosa

## Summary
Art. 798 c.c. governs the donor's liability for hidden defects (vizi) in the donated property. Unless a special agreement provides otherwise, the donor is not responsible for such defects; the only exception is when the donor acted with fraud (dolo). This rule significantly limits the warranty obligation compared to sales contracts, reflecting the gratuitous nature of donations.

## Key Details
- General rule: no warranty for defects in the donated thing without a specific pact.
- Exception: the donor is liable if he was in bad faith (dolo).
- Cross-reference to [[art789]] which concerns warranty against eviction — both articles are part of the same title on donations.
- The article applies to donations regulated by Book II, Title V, Chapter III of the Codice Civile.
- The burden of proving the donor’s fraud rests on the donee.
- In contrast, sales contracts impose an automatic warranty for defects (art. 1490 c.c. et seq.) unless excluded by agreement.
