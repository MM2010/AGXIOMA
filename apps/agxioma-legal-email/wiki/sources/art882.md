# Art. 882 codice civile - Riparazioni del muro comune

## Summary
This article of the Italian Civil Code regulates the allocation of costs for necessary repairs and reconstructions of a common wall (muro comune) among co-owners. Costs must be borne by all parties entitled to the wall, in proportion to their respective rights, unless the need for repair was caused by the fault of one participant. A co-owner may avoid contribution by renouncing their right of common ownership, provided the wall does not support a building belonging to them; however, renunciation does not release the co-owner from liability for repairs caused by their own act.

## Key Details
- Necessary repairs and reconstructions of a common wall are charged to all co-owners in proportion to their right of ownership.
- Exception: if the repair was caused by the act of one participant, that participant bears the entire cost.
- A co-owner may avoid future contribution by renouncing their right of common ownership (rinunzia alla comunione).
- Renunciation is ineffective if the common wall supports a building belonging to the renouncing co-owner (i.e., they cannot escape structural obligations).
- Renunciation does not release the co-owner from liability for repairs already caused by their own fault.
- This rule derives from the general principle of communione (arts. 1100–1116 cc) and modifies the regime of compulsory maintenance of common walls (art. 880 c.c., art. 882 c.c.).
- The article is part of the section on distances in constructions, plantings, excavations, and walls separating properties (Sezione VI, Capo II, Titolo II, Libro III).
