# Art. 518 undecies codice penale - Uscita o esportazione illecite di beni culturali

## Summary
This page documents Article 518 undecies of the Italian Penal Code, part of Title VIII-bis (Crimes against cultural heritage). It criminalizes the unlawful transfer abroad of cultural goods (including items of artistic, historical, archaeological, ethno-anthropological, bibliographic, documentary, or archival interest) without the required certificate of free circulation or export license. The same penalty applies to failure to return temporarily exported cultural goods within the deadline and to making false declarations to avoid export authorization.

## Key Details
- Punishment: imprisonment from 2 to 8 years and a fine of up to €80,000.
- Applies to any cultural good or other object protected under cultural heritage regulations.
- Also punishes failure to re-import temporarily authorized items after the term expires.
- Covers false statements made to the competent export office to prove that an item is not subject to export authorization.
- Inserted into the Penal Code by Legislative Decree No. 42/2004 (Cultural Heritage Code) and subsequent amendments.
