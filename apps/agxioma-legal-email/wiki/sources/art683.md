# Art. 683 codice penale - Pubblicazione delle discussioni o delle deliberazioni segrete di una delle Camere

## Summary
Article 683 of the Italian Penal Code criminalizes the unauthorized publication—through the press or any of the means listed in [[art662]] (including summaries)—of the content of secret discussions or deliberations held by the Senate or the Chamber of Deputies. The offence is a *contravvenzione* (misdemeanour) punishable by arrest up to thirty days or a fine ranging from €51 to €258, provided the act does not constitute a more serious crime. This provision safeguards parliamentary confidentiality and is part of the broader "polizia di sicurezza" (public security policing) framework within the third book of the Penal Code.

## Key Details
- **Legal reference**: Art. 683 c.p. – Book III, Title I, Chapter I, Section III.
- **Offending conduct**: Publishing, even by summary, the content of secret parliamentary discussions or deliberations without authorization.
- **Means of publication**: Those indicated in Art. 662 (e.g., printing, broadcasting, other dissemination methods).
- **Subjects protected**: Discussions or deliberations of the Senate (*Senato della Repubblica*) or the Chamber of Deputies (*Camera dei deputati*) held in secret.
- **Penalty**: Arrest (*arresto*) up to 30 days *or* a fine (*ammenda*) from €51 to €258.
- **Subsidiarity clause**: The article applies only if the act does not constitute a more serious crime (e.g., revealing state secrets under other provisions).
- **Nature**: *Contravvenzione* (non-criminal infraction under the Penal Code's third book), emphasizing administrative/regulatory enforcement.
