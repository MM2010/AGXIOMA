# Art. 482 codice penale - Falsità materiale commessa dal privato

## Summary
Art. 482 of the Italian Penal Code punishes the private individual (or a public official acting outside the exercise of his functions) who commits any of the acts of material falsity described in articles 476, 477 and 478. The applicable penalties are those established for the public official in the said articles, reduced by one third. The provision ensures that the same forms of material forgery are punished even when carried out by private persons, albeit with a lighter penalty.

## Key Details
- **Article number**: 482 c.p. (Codice Penale).
- **Offence**: Material falsity (falsità materiale) committed by a private individual (privato) or by a public official outside the exercise of his functions.
- **Penalty**: The penalties set forth in articles 476, 477 and 478 are applied, reduced by one third.
- **Relation to other articles**: The article refers to the three main forms of material falsity in acts - public document (art. 476), certificate or authorization (art. 477), and transcript or certification (art. 478).
- **Further references**: The article explicitly notes the relevance of articles 490 and 491 (dealing with special cases or equivalent falsities).
- **Scope**: Covers both complete forgeries and alterations performed by private individuals.
- **Systematic position**: Book II, Title VII (Crimes against public faith), Chapter III (Falsity in acts).
