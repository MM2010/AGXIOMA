# Art. 143 bis codice civile - Cognome della moglie

## Summary
The article regulates the surname of the wife after marriage. It provides that the wife adds her husband's surname to her own and retains it during widowhood until she remarries. This provision is part of the rights and duties arising from marriage under Book I, Title VI, Chapter IV of the Italian Civil Code.

## Key Details
- The wife must add her husband's surname to her own upon marriage.
- The added surname is retained during the state of widowhood.
- The obligation ceases if the widow remarries.
- Cross-references within the same code: [[art156-bis]] and [[art328]].
- Located in: Codice Civile, Libro Primo, Titolo VI (Del matrimonio), Capo IV (Dei diritti e dei doveri che nascono dal matrimonio).
