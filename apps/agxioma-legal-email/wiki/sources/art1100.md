# Art. 1100 codice civile - Norme regolatrici

## Summary
Art. 1100 of the Italian Civil Code establishes the residual rule for co-ownership (comunione) of property or other real rights. It provides that, when the same right belongs to several persons, the general norms on co-ownership apply unless the title (e.g., contract, will) or another specific legal provision (such as the Navigation Code articles 258 ff.) states otherwise. This article serves as the gateway to the entire regulation of ordinary co-ownership under Book III, Title VII, Chapter I of the Codice Civile.

## Key Details
- Located in **Codice Civile, Libro III (Della proprietà), Titolo VII (Della comunione), Capo I (Della comunione in generale)**.
- References to applicable exceptions: "se il titolo o la legge non dispone diversamente" (unless the title or the law provides otherwise).
- Explicitly mentions exceptions under the Navigation Code (artt. 258 e ss. cod. nav.) for co-ownership of ships and aircraft.
- Applicable to both ownership and other real rights (e.g., usufruct, servitude) held in common.
- Forms the foundation for the succeeding articles (artt. 1101–1120 c.c.) that detail rights and obligations of co-owners.
- The article does not define "comunione" but assumes the existence of a plurality of subjects holding the same right.
