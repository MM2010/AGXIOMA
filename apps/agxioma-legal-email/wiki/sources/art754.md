# Art. 754 codice civile – Pagamento dei debiti e rivalsa

## Summary
This article of the Italian Civil Code governs the liability of co-heirs for hereditary debts and the right of recourse among them. Heirs are personally liable in proportion to their share and hypothecarily for the whole amount, while a co‑heir who pays more than his portion can only recover from the others the part they are obliged to contribute under art. 752, even if subrogated to the creditors’ rights. The article also preserves the co‑heir’s right to claim a personal credit secured by a mortgage like any other creditor, after deducting his own share.

## Key Details
- Heirs are **personally liable** for hereditary debts and charges **in proportion** to their inheritance share (see [[art1295]]).
- Heirs are **hypothecarily liable for the entire debt** (see [[art2809]] co.2).
- A co‑heir who pays **more than his share** may seek recourse from the other co‑heirs only for the amount they must contribute under [[art752]].
- Recourse is limited **even if** the paying co‑heir is subrogated to the creditors’ rights (see [[art755]], [[art1299]]).
- A co‑heir who is also a **personal creditor** of the deceased and whose credit is secured by a mortgage retains the right to claim payment **like any other creditor**, after deducting his own share as a co‑heir (see [[art752]], [[art1299]]).
