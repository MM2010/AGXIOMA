# Art. 565 codice penale – Attentati alla morale familiare commessi col mezzo della stampa periodica

## Summary
Art. 565 of the Italian Penal Code punishes anyone who, in daily newspapers or other periodical writings, in illustrations related to them, or in paid advertisements placed in the same newspapers or writings, exposes or highlights circumstances that offend family morality. The penalty is a fine ranging from €103 to €516. This provision is part of Title XI (Crimes against the family), Chapter II (Crimes against family morality) and aims to protect the moral integrity of the family from harmful public portrayals in the periodical press.

## Key Details
- Offence: exposing or highlighting circumstances offending family morality through periodical press (newspapers, magazines, periodicals), related illustrations, or advertisements placed therein.
- Subject: any person ("chiunque") who commits the described act.
- Penalty: a fine from €103 to €516 (multa).
- Classified under Libro II, Titolo XI, Capo II of the Italian Penal Code.
- The provision specifically targets the medium of periodical press, distinguishing it from other forms of media or non‑periodical publications.
- It is an offence against family morality, a category that historically protected family values and decency.
