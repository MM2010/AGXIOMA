# Art. 192 codice penale - Atti a titolo gratuito compiuti dal colpevole dopo il reato

## Summary
Article 192 of the Italian Penal Code establishes that gratuitous acts (donations or similar transfers without consideration) performed by the guilty party after the commission of the crime are ineffective against the credits specified in Article 189 of the same code. This provision protects the rights of civil claimants (e.g., victims seeking damages) by preventing the offender from fraudulently dissipating assets through gifts or other gratuitous dispositions after the criminally relevant conduct.

## Key Details
- Applies only to **gratuitous acts** (atti a titolo gratuito) carried out by the offender after the crime.
- The ineffectiveness is limited to the credits referred to in [[art189-cp]] (which governs civil liability for damages arising from the crime).
- The norm directly references [[art2901-codice-civile]] (the civil code’s revocatory action), making the criminal provision coordinate with civil remedy against fraudulent conveyances.
- It does not affect acts for value (onerosi) or acts performed before the crime.
- The provision is placed within the “Sanzioni civili” (civil sanctions) title of Book One of the Penal Code.
- The burden of proving the gratuitous nature and the timing of the act falls on the creditor (civil claimant).
- The rule aims to prevent the offender from rendering insolvent the civil obligation arising from the crime.
