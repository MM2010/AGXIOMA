# Art. 523 codice penale - Ratto a fine di libidine (abrogato)

## Summary
This article of the Italian Penal Code, abrogated by Law 15 February 1996, n. 66, criminalised the abduction or detention of a minor or an adult woman by violence, threat, or deceit for purposes of lust. Its repeal was part of a broader reform that reorganised sexual offences, shifting focus from public morality to the protection of individual sexual freedom.

## Key Details
- **Article text** (abrogated): punished with imprisonment from three to five years whoever, by violence, threat, or deceit, abducts or detains a minor or an adult woman for lewd purposes.
- **Aggravating circumstances**: the penalty was increased if the victim was under eighteen or a married woman.
- **Abrogation**: L. 15 febbraio 1996, n. 66 (intervention against sexual violence, exploitation, and abuse).
- **Context**: belongs to Title IX (crimes against public morality and decency), Chapter I (crimes against sexual liberty) of the 1930 Penal Code.
- **Historical significance**: before the 1996 reform, many sexual offences were framed as offences against morality rather than against the victim's autonomy.
