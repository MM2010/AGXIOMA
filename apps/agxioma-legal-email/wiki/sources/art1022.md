# Art. 1022 codice civile - Abitazione

## Summary
Art. 1022 of the Italian Civil Code defines the right of habitation (*diritto di abitazione*) as a real right allowing the holder to live in a house only to the extent necessary for himself and his family. It is placed within Book III (Property), Title V (Usufruct, Use, and Habitation), Chapter II (Use and Habitation). The article explicitly references the needs of the holder and his family and links to [[art540-cc]] (surviving spouse's right) and [[art1023-cc]] (extent of the right). This right is strictly personal and cannot exceed the user's actual living requirements.

## Key Details
- The right of habitation is a lesser real right, similar to *uso* but limited to dwelling only.
- The holder may inhabit the house only for his own needs and those of his family (as defined in [[art1023-cc]]).
- Express reference to [[art540 comma2-cc]] (the surviving spouse's right of habitation on the family home as part of the legitimate portion).
- The right cannot be transferred or leased beyond personal use.
- The family includes persons living with the holder at the time the right arises, as per [[art1023-cc]].
- Located in the Civil Code: Book III, Title V, Chapter II (articles 1021–1026).
- The right of habitation is typically established by will, law, or contract.
- Related to usufruct (*usufrutto*) but more restrictive – only habitation, not other uses.
